## Method Description

SurveyLens introduces a discipline-aware evaluation framework for automated survey generation. It provides 1,000 surveys across 10 academic disciplines, with the key insight that survey quality criteria and citation conventions vary significantly across fields. A single evaluation rubric applied uniformly across disciplines may favor certain writing patterns over others.

SurveyLens uses a dual-lens evaluation framework: (1) Rubric lens — per-discipline rubrics that capture field-specific quality criteria (e.g., emphasis on experimental detail in biology vs theoretical framing in physics); (2) Canonical alignment lens — measuring how well a generated survey aligns with the canonical structure and coverage expectations of the discipline's survey tradition.

The 10 disciplines span natural sciences, social sciences, engineering, and humanities. For each discipline, domain experts crafted field-specific rubrics and identified canonical reference surveys. SurveyLens enables evaluation of whether a survey system adapts its output to disciplinary norms.
