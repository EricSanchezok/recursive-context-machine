# Paper Profile: DeepSurvey — Enhancing Analytical Depth and Citation Reliability in Automated Survey Generation

- **arXiv ID**: 2605.29522
- **Authors**: Ziyue Yang, Da Ma, Hanqi Li, Zijian Wang, Tiancheng Huang, Zijian Hu, Chenrun Wang, Yunzhe Zhang, Xiaobao Wu, Kai Yu, Lu Chen
- **Year**: 2026

## Method Description
DeepSurvey is an agentic system that addresses two fundamental limitations of existing automated survey generators: shallow analytical depth (reliance on abstracts, isolated paper processing) and unreliable citations (imprecise retrieval, post-hoc grounding). For depth enhancement, DeepSurvey extracts structured keynotes from full-text papers, models cross-paper relationships through clustering and comparative analysis, and integrates code-repository analysis to recover implementation-level details. For citation reliability, it combines citation-graph expansion with hybrid filtering for topic-focussed retrieval, enforces evidence-constrained citation assignment, and deploys multi-granularity agentic refinement to validate citation-claim alignment.

## Architecture
- Structured keynote extraction from full-text PDFs
- Cross-paper relationship modeling via clustering + comparative analysis
- Code-repository analysis integration
- Citation-graph expansion with hybrid filtering
- Evidence-constrained citation assignment
- Multi-granularity agentic refinement (paragraph-level, section-level, document-level)

## Key Innovations
- First system to combine citation-graph expansion with multi-granularity refinement for citation reliability
- Achieves citation quality gains (12.3% recall, 9.3% precision) over strongest baseline
- Systematic evaluation across multiple domains with robustness analysis
- Domain experts prefer DeepSurvey over human-written surveys (83.3% overall quality, 100% content depth)

## Method Category
`multi_agent_pipeline` with strong `graph_enhanced_retrieval` characteristics. Uses citation-graph expansion as a core reliability mechanism, combined with multi-agent refinement. Bridges Phase 5 (graph re-integration) with Phase 3 (multi-agent coordination).

## Relevance to Knowledge Gap
Directly addresses two gaps: (1) shallow analytical depth — DeepSurvey's structured keynote extraction and cross-paper modeling demonstrate that critical-analytic depth can be measured and improved; (2) citation reliability — evidence-constrained citation assignment and multi-granularity refinement show a path beyond the bottleneck transfer problem. Provides concrete evidence that graph-enhanced retrieval combined with multi-agent refinement produces measurable quality improvements across both content and citation dimensions.
