## Method Description

Text2Grad introduces a method for extracting span-level gradients from natural language feedback for interpretable policy optimization. Instead of using a scalar reward signal, Text2Grad parses textual feedback to identify which specific spans or aspects of the output contributed to positive or negative evaluation. These span-level "gradients" provide fine-grained, interpretable signals for policy updates.

The approach works by: (1) collecting natural language feedback on model outputs, (2) parsing feedback to extract span-level positive and negative signals, (3) using these signals to compute gradient estimates for policy optimization, and (4) updating the policy in a direction that preserves positively-evaluated spans while modifying negatively-evaluated ones.
