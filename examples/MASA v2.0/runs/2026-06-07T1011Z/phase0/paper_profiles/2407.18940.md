## Description

LitSearch is a retrieval benchmark for scientific literature search, containing 597 carefully constructed queries with expert-judged relevance judgments. It evaluates the ability of retrieval systems to find relevant papers for complex scientific information needs.

The queries are designed to test: (1) Simple fact queries — finding papers that report specific findings; (2) Method queries — finding papers using specific methodologies; (3) Comparative queries — finding papers comparing specific approaches; (4) Synthesis queries — finding papers that together support a broader claim. Each query has expert-annotated relevant papers.

LitSearch is directly relevant for evaluating the retrieval component of survey generation systems — before a survey can be written, relevant papers must be discovered. The query taxonomy (fact, method, comparative, synthesis) aligns with the types of retrieval needed for different survey sections.
