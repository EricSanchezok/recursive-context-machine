## Method Description

CitationSum uses citation-aware training objectives for scientific paper summarization. The method constructs a citation graph where papers are nodes and citation relationships are edges. A graph contrastive learning objective trains the model to generate summaries that capture citation-relevant content — information that is useful for understanding a paper's contribution relative to the papers it cites and that cite it.

The contrastive objective pulls together summaries of papers that are strongly citation-coupled and pushes apart summaries of unrelated papers. This produces summaries that highlight a paper's novel contribution, its relationship to prior work, and its influence on later work — information that is critical for survey generation where papers need to be described in context, not in isolation.

## Category Rationale

CitationSum uses the citation graph as a training signal for summarization through contrastive learning. The graph provides the supervisory signal, making it graph_enhanced_retrieval.

## Limitations

- Summarizes individual papers, not surveys
- Citation-aware training requires a complete citation graph
- Contrastive learning may not capture all relevant citation relationships
- Summary quality depends on citation graph coverage
