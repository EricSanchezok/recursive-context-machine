## Method Description

SPAR (Scholar Paper Retrieval) uses RefChain-based query decomposition with LLM-based agents, evaluated on the SPARBench benchmark. The RefChain approach recursively decomposes complex literature search queries into simpler sub-queries, retrieves results for each sub-query, and synthesizes a final result set. The decomposition is guided by the LLM's understanding of what types of evidence would satisfy the query.

The RefChain enables the system to handle complex multi-faceted queries like "find papers that compare Method A and Method B on Dataset C" by breaking them into sub-queries for Method A papers, Method B papers, and Dataset C papers, then finding the intersection. SPARBench provides a standardized evaluation for literature search systems.

## Limitations

- Focused on search/retrieval, not survey generation
- No citation graph traversal for structural discovery
- RefChain decomposition quality depends on LLM capability
- May not handle queries that require diverse evidence types
