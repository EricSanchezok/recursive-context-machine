## Method Description

S2ORC (Semantic Scholar Open Research Corpus) is a large-scale corpus of 8.1M academic papers from Semantic Scholar, featuring full-text parsing, citation extraction, and structured metadata. The corpus includes: (1) full-text of papers with sections, paragraphs, and sentences, (2) extracted citation relationships with citation context (which sentences cite which papers), (3) paper metadata (title, authors, venue, year, fields of study), and (4) entity annotations for methods, datasets, and tasks.

The corpus is a foundational resource for citation-aware NLP and survey generation research, providing both text content and citation structure for academic papers. The citation context annotations enable training models that understand how papers are cited — whether for methodology, comparison, or foundation — which is critical for faithful citation in generated surveys.
