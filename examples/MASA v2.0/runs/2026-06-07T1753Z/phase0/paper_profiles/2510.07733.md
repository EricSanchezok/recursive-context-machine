## Method Description

SurveyG is a survey generation framework built on a hierarchical citation graph with three layers: Foundation (seminal/landmark papers), Development (papers that extend or build on foundations), and Frontier (recent cutting-edge work). The framework uses both horizontal traversal (within a layer — finding papers at the same hierarchical level) and vertical traversal (between layers — moving from foundations through developments to frontiers) to generate surveys that provide a structured view of the research landscape.

The generation pipeline: (1) constructs the three-layer citation graph from topic-retrieved papers, (2) traverses horizontally within each layer to identify the key papers, (3) traverses vertically to trace the evolution of ideas, and (4) generates the survey structured by the three-layer hierarchy. SurveyG is specifically designed to address the citation graph traversal gap — it is the most directly relevant paper to the survey topic of citation-graph-driven survey generation.

- Horizontal and vertical traversal depth may need manual tuning
- Does not incorporate iterative refinement based on feedback
