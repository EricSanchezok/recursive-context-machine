## Method Description

R3 (Read, Revise, Repeat) is a system for human-in-the-loop iterative text revision with minimal effort design. The system presents text to a human reviewer who can accept or reject sentences or paragraphs with a single click. The AI then revises the rejected portions based on context, re-reading the entire text to maintain coherence after each revision.

The key design principle is minimizing human effort — rather than requiring humans to write edits, the system only requires accept/reject judgments. The AI handles all actual editing, using the accept/reject signals to understand which parts need improvement. This is directly applicable to survey revision: a human reviewer could quickly flag problematic sections (coverage gaps, citation errors, unclear writing) while the AI handles the actual revision work.

## Category Rationale

R3 places the human in the loop as an evaluator who provides accept/reject feedback, while the AI performs all revision work. The interactive cycle with human oversight qualifies it as hybrid_interactive.

## Limitations

- Binary accept/reject feedback may not capture nuanced quality issues
- AI revision quality depends on understanding the reason for rejection
- Does not address literature retrieval or citation concerns
- Single-sentence granularity may not work well for survey-level revision
