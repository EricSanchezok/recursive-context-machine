## Description

LLM4SR is a comprehensive survey of how large language models are being applied across the scientific research lifecycle, including literature review, hypothesis generation, experimental design, data analysis, and paper writing. It provides a structured taxonomy of LLM applications in scientific research.

The survey categorizes LLM capabilities for: (1) Literature understanding — paper retrieval, summarization, citation analysis; (2) Knowledge synthesis — survey generation, related-work writing, evidence synthesis; (3) Research ideation — hypothesis generation, gap identification; (4) Writing assistance — draft generation, citation formatting, language polishing. For each category, it reviews existing systems, benchmarks, and limitations.

As a reference work, LLM4SR provides context for understanding where survey generation fits within the broader landscape of LLM applications for scientific research.
