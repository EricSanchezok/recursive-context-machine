## Description

This paper evaluates GPT-4's ability to generate Wikipedia-style survey articles on 99 NLP topics, providing an early assessment of LLM capabilities for structured survey writing. It compares LLM-generated articles against human-written Wikipedia articles and analyzes human vs GPT evaluation bias.

The methodology: (1) Generate survey articles on 99 NLP topics using GPT-4; (2) Evaluate using both human annotators and GPT-4 as judge; (3) Compare evaluation results to identify systematic biases. Key findings include: GPT-4 generates coherent, well-structured survey articles that are competitive with human-written ones on many topics; human evaluators show preference for human-written text even when GPT-4 output is objectively similar; GPT-4-as-judge shows systematic bias toward preferring its own outputs.

The paper is significant for identifying evaluation methodology challenges — particularly the bias in LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation — that remain relevant for current survey generation evaluation.
