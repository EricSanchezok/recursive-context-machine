## Method Description

This systematic review examines generative multi-agent collaboration in embodied AI (EMAS — Embodied Multi-Agent Systems). It provides a taxonomy of collaboration mechanisms across four dimensions: perception (how agents perceive each other and the environment), planning (how agents coordinate actions), communication (protocols and message formats), and feedback analysis (how agents learn from outcomes).

While focused on embodied AI (robotics, physical agents), the collaboration patterns — particularly communication protocols, shared planning, and feedback-based learning — are transferable to software-based multi-agent systems for survey generation. The paper catalogs how agents decompose tasks, assign roles, share information, and resolve conflicts in decentralized settings.
