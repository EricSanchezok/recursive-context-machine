## Method Description

SurveyGen is a quality-aware RAG pipeline for survey generation, trained on a dataset of 4,200+ human-written surveys. The system uses: (1) quality-aware retrieval that prefers papers from high-quality venues and with high citation impact, (2) RAG-based section generation with citation-grounded claims, and (3) a quality estimation module that predicts survey quality dimensions (coverage, accuracy, readability) without requiring human evaluation.

The quality estimation module enables iterative improvement — the system can identify weak sections and regenerate them. The training on 4,200+ human surveys provides a strong foundation for generating surveys that match human writing patterns and quality standards. The quality-aware retrieval incorporates prestige signals (venue quality, citation count) alongside relevance.

## Category Rationale

SurveyGen operates as a single quality-aware RAG pipeline with automated quality estimation. Despite the quality-awareness and iterative refinement, it is a single-agent pipeline without multi-agent coordination.

## Limitations

- No citation graph traversal — relies on quality-weighted retrieval
- Prestige-based quality weighting may introduce Matthew effect bias
- Training on 4,200 surveys may not cover all survey types and domains
- Quality estimation accuracy depends on training data coverage
