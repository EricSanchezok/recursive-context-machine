## SurveyBench Evaluation Dimensions (From Paper)

### A. Outline Quality (30% Weight)

**A1. Coverage Breadth** (1-5)
- 1: Covers only a narrow subset; major research directions missing
- 2: Covers some areas but significant gaps in important sub-topics
- 3: Covers most major research directions with minor omissions
- 4: Broad coverage; all key sub-areas represented
- 5: Exceptionally comprehensive; covers full landscape including emerging directions

**A2. Logical Coherence** (1-5)
- 1: No clear organizational logic; sections randomly ordered
- 2: Weak logical flow; transitions abrupt or unclear
- 3: Generally coherent structure; most sections follow logically
- 4: Well-organized with clear progression; categories meaningful and non-overlapping
- 5: Elegant structure building understanding progressively; taxonomy insightful and natural

### B. Content Quality (50% Weight)

**B1. Synthesis Granularity** (1-5)
- 1: Mere listing without synthesis; no comparison or grouping
- 2: Basic grouping but comparisons are superficial
- 3: Meaningful synthesis with reasonable comparison across papers
- 4: Detailed synthesis with nuanced comparisons; strengths/weaknesses clearly articulated
- 5: Expert-level synthesis identifying subtle distinctions, trade-offs, and evolution of ideas

**B2. Clarity of Insights** (1-5)
- 1: No original insights; purely descriptive
- 2: Few insights; mostly paraphrasing individual paper contributions
- 3: Some cross-cutting insights beyond individual papers
- 4: Multiple original insights identifying trends, gaps, and tensions
- 5: Deep, novel insights valuable to domain experts; identifies blind spots and unexamined assumptions

### C. Non-textual Richness & Reference Quality (20% Weight)

**C1. Reference Relevance & Coverage** (1-5)
- 1: References sparse or mostly irrelevant
- 2: Some relevant references but key works missing
- 3: Adequate reference coverage; most important works cited
- 4: Comprehensive reference set covering seminal and recent works
- 5: Expert-curated references; appropriate balance of seminal, recent, and niche contributions

**C2. Non-textual Elements** (1-5)
- 1: No figures, tables, or structured comparisons
- 2: Minimal use of tables or figures; mostly text
- 3: Some comparative tables or figures aiding understanding
- 4: Well-designed tables and figures effectively summarizing comparisons
- 5: Excellent use of taxonomies, comparison tables, and diagrams substantially enhancing comprehension
