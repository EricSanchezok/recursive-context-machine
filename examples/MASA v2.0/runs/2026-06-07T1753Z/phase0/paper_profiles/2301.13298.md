## Method Description

LongEval provides gold-standard human evaluation guidelines for assessing faithfulness in long-form summarization. Based on a 162-paper survey of evaluation practices, it proposes a clause-level annotation protocol where evaluators mark each clause in a generated summary as "supported," "unsupported but consistent with source," or "unsupported/contradictory." Specific guidelines are provided for handling common challenges: evaluating claims not explicitly stated but inferable from the source, handling aggregate claims (e.g., "most papers find..."), and assessing numerical accuracy.

The annotation protocol is designed for clause-level granularity, enabling precise identification of hallucination and faithfulness issues. This evaluation methodology is directly applicable to survey generation evaluation, where each claim about the literature must be faithful to the cited papers. The guidelines provide a rigorous, replicable protocol for assessing citation faithfulness.

## Limitations

- Human evaluation protocol — expensive to scale
- Clause-level annotation is time-intensive for surveys
- Does not address coverage or comprehensiveness — only faithfulness
- Protocol designed for summarization, may need adaptation for surveys
