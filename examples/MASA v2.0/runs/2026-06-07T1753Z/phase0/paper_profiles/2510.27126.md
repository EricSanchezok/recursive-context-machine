## Method Description

AURA (Adaptive Understanding and Response Agent) applies reinforcement learning (epsilon-greedy policy) to adaptively conduct conversational surveys. The system learns to dynamically adjust its questioning strategy based on user responses, using RL to balance exploration (asking new types of questions) with exploitation (asking questions that have proven effective). An LSDE (Learned Survey Dynamics Estimator) metric tracks survey quality and informs policy updates.

While designed for conversational surveys (questionnaire-type surveys with human respondents), the RL-based adaptive framework is directly transferable to literature survey generation. An RL policy could learn to adapt literature search and synthesis strategies based on intermediate quality signals — dynamically deciding when to expand citation depth, when to switch topics, or when to refine a section for better coverage.

## Limitations

- Designed for conversational (human-respondent) surveys, not literature surveys
- No citation graph or literature retrieval capabilities
- LSDE metric may not transfer directly to literature survey quality
- RL policy requires online interaction data for training
