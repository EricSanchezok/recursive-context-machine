## Method Description

This is the only dedicated survey on citation recommendation approaches and datasets. It taxonomizes citation recommendation methods into: (1) content-based methods (using paper text), (2) graph-based methods (using citation structure), (3) hybrid methods combining both, and (4) context-aware methods (considering where in the paper the citation is needed).

The survey catalogs available datasets for citation recommendation evaluation, their sizes, domains, and evaluation protocols. It identifies open challenges including cold-start recommendation (for new papers without citation history), cross-domain recommendation, and temporal dynamics of citation relevance.
