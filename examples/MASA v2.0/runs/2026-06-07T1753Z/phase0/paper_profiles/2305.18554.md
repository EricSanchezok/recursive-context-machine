## Method Description

This paper examines citational amnesia in NLP by analyzing 71,500 NLP papers, finding that 62% of citations come from the immediate 5 years and that temporal diversity in citations is at an all-time low. The study categorizes citation functions (foundational, comparative, methodological, decorative) and shows that recency bias affects each category differently — foundational citations show the strongest age decline while methodological citations maintain more stable age distributions.

The paper attributes the trend to multiple factors: the exponential growth of NLP publications (more recent papers to cite), the pressure to cite contemporaneous work, the design of search engines that prioritize recent papers, and the implicit bias in peer review toward recent citations. The findings provide a baseline for evaluating whether automated survey systems reproduce or amplify human recency bias.
