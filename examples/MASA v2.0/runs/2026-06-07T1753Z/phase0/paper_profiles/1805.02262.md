## Method Description

This paper describes the construction pipeline for the Semantic Scholar literature graph — a large-scale heterogeneous graph of 280M+ academic papers, authors, venues, and their relationships. The pipeline processes incoming papers through: (1) extraction of metadata (title, authors, venue, year) from PDFs and metadata sources, (2) citation parsing to extract citation relationships from paper reference lists, (3) author disambiguation using graph-based clustering, (4) field-of-study assignment using hierarchical classification, and (5) entity linking to external knowledge bases.

The resulting literature graph serves as the foundational infrastructure for many downstream survey systems that rely on Semantic Scholar's citation graph for retrieval and expansion. Understanding the graph construction pipeline is essential for assessing the quality and coverage of citation-based retrieval.
