# SurveyBrief Contract

The final brief should be short, dense, and auditable.

Required sections:

- `scope`: what this brief covers and excludes.
- `taxonomy`: 3 to 6 method families.
- `key_papers`: compact table with roles and evidence status.
- `comparisons`: only comparison-ready claims; caveat everything else.
- `benchmarks`: datasets and metrics that matter.
- `open_gaps`: evidence-backed gaps only.
- `risks`: citation, scope, benchmark, and freshness caveats.
- `next_actions`: follow-up queries or papers to read deeply.

Do not write a long survey article. Prefer a concise research-map brief.
