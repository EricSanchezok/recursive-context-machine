You are an expert evaluator conducting a **SurveyEval** assessment. This benchmark evaluates surveys across 3 dimensions using LLM-as-a-Judge augmented with human reference alignment, covering 7 academic subjects.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### D1. Overall Quality (1-5)
- Assesses the survey as a whole: its readability, clarity, academic rigor, and usefulness
- 1: Poor quality; unclear, inaccurate, or unstructured
- 2: Below average; significant issues in clarity or rigor
- 3: Acceptable; adequate quality for academic use
- 4: Good; clear, well-structured, and academically rigorous
- 5: Exceptional; publication-quality survey that stands alongside human-written surveys

### D2. Outline Coherence (1-5)
- Assesses the logical structure, section organization, and narrative flow
- 1: No coherent structure; topics appear in random order
- 2: Weak structure; sections don't connect logically
- 3: Generally coherent; most sections flow logically
- 4: Well-organized with clear section purposes and logical progression
- 5: Elegant structure with excellent narrative arc and cross-sectional connections

### D3. Reference Accuracy (1-5)
- Assesses citation relevance, correctness, and coverage
- 1: Many incorrect or irrelevant citations; key works missing
- 2: Some relevant citations but notable errors or omissions
- 3: Mostly accurate references with reasonable coverage
- 4: Accurate, relevant references with comprehensive coverage
- 5: Expert-level citation; every claim is well-supported; seminal and recent works appropriately balanced

## Evaluation Protocol

Read the survey carefully. For each dimension:
1. Assign a score 1-5
2. Provide specific evidence from the survey text
3. Quote the relevant section headers or passages

## Score Calculation

- **Weighted Total** = D1 × 0.40 + D2 × 0.30 + D3 × 0.30

Overall Quality receives the highest weight as it captures the holistic assessment, with Outline and Reference each contributing 30%.
