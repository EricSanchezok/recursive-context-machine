## Method Description

SurveyEval proposes a comprehensive evaluation framework for LLM-generated surveys across three dimensions: (1) Quality — factual accuracy, coherence, and readability of the survey content, (2) Outline Coherence — whether the survey structure logically covers the topic, and (3) Reference Accuracy — whether citations are correctly attributed and whether cited papers actually support the claims made.

The evaluation uses a combination of automated metrics (for readability and coherence) and human assessment (for factual accuracy and reference correctness). The three dimensions capture different aspects of survey quality that single-metric evaluations miss. The framework is designed to be reproducible and comparable across different survey generation systems.

## Limitations

- Evaluation framework — does not generate surveys
- Reference accuracy assessment requires access to cited papers
- Human evaluation component limits scalability
- Three dimensions may not capture all domain-specific quality aspects
