## Method Description

NLP Scholar is an interactive visual exploration tool for the NLP literature that uses citation-aware visualization. The system processes NLP papers and their citation relationships to create an interactive visualization where papers are positioned based on citation similarity (papers that are strongly citation-coupled appear close together). Users can explore the literature landscape, identify clusters of related work, and trace citation trajectories.

The tool combines citation graph analysis with interactive visualization, enabling researchers to visually understand the structure of a research field — which papers are central, how research areas cluster, and how ideas propagate through citations. The visualization is driven by citation-based similarity rather than text content alone.
