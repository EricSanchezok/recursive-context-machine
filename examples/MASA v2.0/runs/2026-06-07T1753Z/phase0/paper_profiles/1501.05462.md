## Method Description

This comprehensive survey of theory and practice in scientometrics covers citation metrics, normalization methods, and evaluation frameworks. The paper reviews: (1) fundamental citation metrics (citation count, h-index, impact factor), (2) normalization methods (field-normalized citations, percentile-based indicators), (3) citation network analysis (co-citation, bibliographic coupling, citation graph properties), and (4) evaluation frameworks (research evaluation, university rankings, funding decisions).

The survey provides foundational knowledge for understanding citation-based indicators and their limitations. This is directly relevant to survey systems that use citation metrics for paper ranking, quality estimation, or relevance assessment. Understanding the theory and practice of scientometrics helps designers avoid misusing citation metrics.
