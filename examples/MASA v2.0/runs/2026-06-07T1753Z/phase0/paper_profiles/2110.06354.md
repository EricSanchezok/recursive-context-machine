## Method Description

This paper presents a graph-optimization-based method for generating reading paths through scientific literature and introduces the SurveyBank dataset. Given a topic, the method constructs a citation graph of relevant papers and then finds an optimal reading path — a sequence of papers that provides a structured introduction to the topic, covering foundational works first and progressing to advanced results.

The reading path is optimized to minimize the total "cognitive load" by ensuring each paper builds on concepts introduced in earlier papers in the path. The SurveyBank dataset provides a large-scale collection of CS survey papers with citation relationships, enabling training and evaluation of reading path generation and survey writing systems.

## Limitations

- Reading paths are sequential — may not capture multi-branch topic structure
- Optimization assumes papers are read in a fixed order, which may not suit all readers
- Does not generate survey text — only reading paths
- SurveyBank dataset may cover limited domains
