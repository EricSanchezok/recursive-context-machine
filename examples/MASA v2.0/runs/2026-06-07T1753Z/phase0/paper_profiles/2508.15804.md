## Method Description

ReportBench is a benchmark for evaluating deep research agents through academic survey generation, focusing on citation quality and faithfulness. The benchmark uses an agent-based automated evaluation approach where an LLM evaluator assesses generated surveys against predefined quality criteria. The evaluation dimensions include: citation accuracy (whether each claim is supported by the cited paper), citation appropriateness (whether the cited paper is the correct source for the claim), citation coverage (whether all claims are supported), and overall faithfulness.

The agent-based evaluation replaces expensive human evaluation with automated LLM assessment, enabling scalable evaluation. The focus on citation quality addresses a critical challenge in automated survey generation — ensuring claims are accurately attributed to supporting papers.

## Limitations

- Agent-based evaluation may inherit LLM biases
- Citation quality evaluation requires access to cited papers
- Focus on citations may underweight other quality dimensions (coverage, readability)
- Automated evaluation may not capture nuanced citation issues
