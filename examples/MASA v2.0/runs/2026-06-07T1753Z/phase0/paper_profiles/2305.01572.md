## Method Description

H2CGL (Hierarchical Heterogeneous Contrastive Graph Learning) proposes a dynamics-aware GNN with contrastive learning and hard negative mining for citation impact prediction. The method models the citation network as a heterogeneous graph with multiple node types (papers, authors, venues, topics) and edge types (citations, authorship, venue-publication). A hierarchical GNN architecture processes information at multiple levels: node-level (individual papers), type-level (aggregating within node types), and graph-level (global context).

The training uses contrastive learning with carefully constructed hard negatives — papers that are structurally similar but have different citation impact trajectories — to learn discriminative embeddings. The dynamics-aware component models how the citation graph changes over time, updating paper embeddings as new citations accrue. This enables prediction of which papers will have sustained vs. declining impact.

## Limitations

- Complex architecture with multiple entity types may be difficult to train and tune
- Hard negative mining introduces additional computational cost
- Impact prediction is a different task from literature retrieval or survey generation
- Does not incorporate text content for semantic understanding
