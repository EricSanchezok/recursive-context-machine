## Method Description

AiReview is an open-source platform that bridges LLM-assisted screening with medical systematic reviews. The platform implements a pipeline for: (1) importing search results from bibliographic databases, (2) LLM-based screening of titles and abstracts against inclusion/exclusion criteria, (3) human validation of LLM screening decisions, and (4) tracking of screening progress with visualizations.

The platform is designed specifically for medical systematic reviews, integrating with existing SR workflows and tools. It is the first open platform to bridge LLM-assisted screening with the medical SR domain, providing transparency into LLM decision-making and enabling human oversight at scale.
