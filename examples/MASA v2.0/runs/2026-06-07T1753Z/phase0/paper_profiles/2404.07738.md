## Method Description

ResearchAgent is an iterative system for generating research ideas grounded in scientific literature. It combines an academic graph (knowledge graph of papers, concepts, and relationships) with a knowledge store (detailed paper content) to support iterative idea refinement through collaborative reviewing agents. The pipeline works as follows: (1) given a research topic, it retrieves relevant papers from the academic graph, (2) an idea generator LLM proposes novel research directions based on the retrieved literature, (3) multiple reviewer LLM agents critique the proposed ideas from different perspectives (novelty, feasibility, significance, grounding in literature), and (4) the idea is refined based on reviewer feedback, iterating through multiple rounds.

The academic graph provides structured relationships between papers (citations, co-citations, shared concepts), enabling the system to identify gaps, contradictions, and unexplored connections in the literature. The collaborative reviewing mechanism mirrors academic peer review, providing multi-perspective evaluation of generated ideas.

## Category Rationale

ResearchAgent employs multiple specialized LLM agents (one generator + multiple reviewers) with distinct roles that coordinate through iterative feedback loops. The reviewers evaluate from different perspectives and the generator refines based on feedback, making it a clear multi_agent_pipeline.

## Limitations

- Focused on research idea generation, not survey generation per se
- Reviewer agent quality depends on prompt design and LLM capability
- Academic graph construction and maintenance is resource-intensive
- May converge on safe, consensus ideas rather than truly novel ones
