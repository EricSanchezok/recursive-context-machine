## Method Description

AutoResearcher is a multi-stage framework for automating research ideation with explicit intermediate reasoning states. The pipeline consists of five stages: (1) Curation — retrieving and organizing relevant literature from scientific databases, (2) Generation — producing candidate research ideas grounded in the curated literature, (3) Selection — ranking and filtering ideas based on quality criteria, (4) Review — evaluating selected ideas against literature grounding and novelty, and (5) Output — producing a structured research proposal with citations.

Each stage produces explicit intermediate outputs (curated paper lists, candidate idea statements, selection rankings, review critiques) that provide transparency into the reasoning process. The framework emphasizes knowledge grounding — every generated idea must cite specific papers as evidence, preventing hallucinated or ungrounded proposals. The multi-stage design with explicit intermediate states enables human oversight and intervention at any stage.

## Category Rationale

AutoResearcher uses five distinct stages with specialized LLM roles (Curator, Generator, Selector, Reviewer, Output Formatter) operating in sequence. Each stage has a distinct function, qualifying it as a multi_agent_pipeline with sequential coordination.

## Limitations

- No citation graph traversal for literature discovery
- Multi-stage pipeline may propagate errors from early stages
- Significant computational cost from multiple LLM calls per idea
- Focused on research ideation rather than comprehensive survey generation
