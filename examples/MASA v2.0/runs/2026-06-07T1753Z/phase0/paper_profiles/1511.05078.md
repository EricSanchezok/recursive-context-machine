## Method Description

This paper compares three types of citation analysis for generating taxonomies of scientific fields: direct citation, bibliographic coupling, and co-citation analysis. The study evaluates which method produces taxonomies that best match expert-created classifications. The finding: direct citation analysis generates the most accurate taxonomies, outperforming bibliographic coupling and co-citation.

This finding has implications for survey systems that use citation relationships to organize literature into taxonomic structures. Direct citation relationships provide the most reliable signal for hierarchical organization, though combining multiple citation relationship types may be beneficial for specific use cases.
