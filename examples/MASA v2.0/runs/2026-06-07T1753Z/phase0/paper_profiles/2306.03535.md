## Method Description

SciLit is an end-to-end assistive writing platform that provides literature discovery, summarization, and abstractive citation generation. The platform integrates: (1) literature search and discovery via embedding similarity, (2) paper summarization using LLMs, (3) abstractive citation generation — generating natural language descriptions of a paper's contribution with inline citations, and (4) assistive writing support for drafting related work sections.

The abstractive citation generation is a key feature: rather than simply citing a paper by ID, SciLit generates contextual descriptions of the paper's contribution (e.g., "Smith et al. (2023) proposed a method for...") that can be inserted into draft text. This bridges the gap between retrieval and natural writing.
