## Method Description

IterSurvey introduces a recurrent outline generation architecture with a dedicated planning agent that iteratively refines the survey outline based on discovered papers. The workflow alternates between planning and writing phases: papers are gathered via search and citation traversal, a planning agent generates or revises the outline, then sections are drafted with strict grounding in "paper cards" — structured summaries of each cited paper that ensure faithful attribution.

The paper cards are a key design element: each time a paper is cited, the system creates a structured card containing the paper's title, key claims, method, and findings. These cards are stored and reused, preventing hallucinated citations and ensuring that the same paper is described consistently throughout the survey. The planning agent uses these cards to verify that each claim in the outline has supporting evidence.

Survey-Arena is the companion benchmark component — a pairwise comparison framework that complements absolute quality scoring. Rather than scoring surveys on absolute scales, Survey-Arena presents evaluators (human or LLM) with two surveys on the same topic and asks which is better, producing more reliable comparative rankings.

## Category Rationale

IterSurvey's core innovation is the iterative planning-writing loop where the outline is recurrently refined based on discovered paper content. This iterative approach to outline generation (starting from a coarse plan and refining it through multiple rounds of paper analysis) is the defining architectural feature. While it uses multiple agents (Planning and Writing), the emphasis is on the refinement loop rather than multi-agent specialization.

## Limitations

- BFS-level citation graph traversal — no hierarchical organization of papers
- Paper cards ensure faithful attribution but don't enable claim-level verification of cited content
- Iterative refinement increases total LLM calls and cost per survey
- Survey-Arena pairwise comparisons don't provide absolute quality scores
