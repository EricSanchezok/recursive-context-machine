## Method Description

SurveyBench is a benchmark for evaluating LLM-written surveys with 11,343 arXiv topics and 4,947 generated surveys, using a quiz-driven evaluation methodology. The evaluation works by: (1) generating a survey on a topic, (2) creating a quiz based on the survey content, (3) having human experts answer the quiz, and (4) measuring how well the survey enables quiz completion. This "quiz-driven" approach assesses whether the survey effectively communicates the topic to readers.

The benchmark includes diverse topics from arXiv categories, enabling comprehensive evaluation across research domains. The human evaluation component provides gold-standard quality assessment, while the quiz format measures practical utility rather than surface-level metrics.
