## Method Description

MATC (Multi-Agent Taskforce Collaboration) introduces a manager + 4 executor multi-agent architecture specifically designed to mitigate compounding errors in automated literature review generation. The architecture consists of: (1) a Manager agent that coordinates the overall process, decomposes the review task, and assigns sub-tasks to executor agents, and (2) four specialized executor agents organized into taskforces: an Exploitation Taskforce (focuses on in-depth analysis of retrieved papers), an Exploration Taskforce (searches for additional relevant literature beyond initial retrieval), an Experience Taskforce (maintains a memory of past errors and successful strategies), and a Self-Correction Taskforce (identifies and fixes errors in intermediate outputs).

The key innovation is the explicit error-mitigation architecture. Each taskforce monitors for specific types of errors: the Exploration taskforce prevents coverage errors (missing relevant papers), the Exploitation taskforce prevents shallow analysis, the Experience taskforce prevents repeated mistakes, and the Self-Correction taskforce catches and fixes errors before they compound. The agents communicate via structured messages with error-tracking metadata.

## Limitations

- Error-tracking metadata adds communication overhead
- Taskforce specialization may create coordination bottlenecks
