## Method Description

MACRec is a multi-agent collaboration framework for product recommendation with five specialized agents: Manager (coordinates the process), Analyst (analyzes user preferences and product features), Reflector (evaluates and refines recommendations), Searcher (retrieves candidate products), and Interpreter (explains recommendations to users).

While designed for product recommendation, the agent roles map to survey generation tasks: Manager (coordinates survey writing), Analyst (analyzes retrieved literature), Reflector (evaluates survey quality), Searcher (retrieves relevant papers), and Interpreter (explains findings). The framework serves as a methodological reference for designing survey-specific multi-agent systems.

## Category Rationale

MACRec employs five specialized agents (Manager, Analyst, Reflector, Searcher, Interpreter) with distinct roles coordinated by a Manager. This qualifies it as a multi_agent_pipeline, even though it is domain-specific to product recommendation.

## Limitations

- Domain-specific to product recommendation, not literature survey
- No citation graph or academic retrieval capabilities
- Agent roles designed for product features, not scientific literature analysis
- Limited evaluation on product recommendation tasks only
