## Method Description

PaSa is an LLM agent for comprehensive academic paper search that combines RL training with synthetic data generation, achieving +37.78% recall@20 over GPT-4o. The agent: (1) takes a research topic or query, (2) iteratively searches for relevant papers using both keyword and citation-based strategies, (3) evaluates discovered papers for relevance, and (4) decides when to stop searching.

The RL training optimizes the agent's search policy — learning which search strategies (following citations, keyword refinement, author-based search) are most effective for finding relevant papers. Synthetic data generation creates training examples of effective search trajectories. The +37.78% recall improvement demonstrates the value of learned search strategies over static retrieval.

## Limitations

- Focused on search/retrieval, not survey generation
- RL training requires search trajectory data
- Synthetic data may not cover all real-world search scenarios
- Citation-based search is one of multiple strategies, not the primary focus
