## Description

This paper provides a comprehensive survey of AI applications for literature review automation, covering both traditional SLR tools (screening, data extraction) and LLM-based approaches (survey generation, citation-aware writing). It systematically reviews 21 traditional SLR tools and 11 LLM-based systems.

The survey categorizes AI assistance across the SLR pipeline: (1) Search and retrieval — automated query formulation, database searching; (2) Screening — relevance classification, priority ranking; (3) Extraction — structured data extraction from full text; (4) Synthesis — narrative generation, evidence tables; (5) Maintenance — living review updates. For each stage, it identifies the best-performing AI methods and remaining challenges.

The paper serves as a bridging reference between traditional SLR methodology and LLM-based approaches. It is relevant as a reference work for understanding the full landscape of AI-assisted literature review.
