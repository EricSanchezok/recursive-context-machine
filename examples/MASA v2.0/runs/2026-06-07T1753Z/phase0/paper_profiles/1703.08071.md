## Method Description

This paper proposes methods for quantifying and suppressing ranking bias in citation networks using Mahalanobis distance and z-score normalization for field and age bias. The key insight: raw citation counts are biased by field size (larger fields generate more citations), paper age (older papers have more time to accumulate citations), and citation practices (some fields cite more heavily than others).

The proposed normalization methods adjust citation counts for these biases, producing fairer rankings of paper impact. The Mahalanobis distance accounts for correlations between bias factors, while z-score normalization adjusts for within-field citation distributions. These methods are relevant for survey systems that use citation-based ranking to prioritize papers.
