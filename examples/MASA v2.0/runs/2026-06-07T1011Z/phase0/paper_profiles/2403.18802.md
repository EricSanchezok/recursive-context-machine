## Description

LongFact provides a dataset of 38 topics requiring long-form factual responses, and SAFE (Search-Augmented Factuality Evaluator) introduces a method for automatically evaluating factuality of long-form text by decomposing claims and verifying them via web search.

SAFE works by: (1) Decomposing generated text into atomic facts; (2) Converting each fact into a search query; (3) Executing the search against a knowledge corpus (web or reference set); (4) Using an LLM to judge whether search results support the original fact. SAFE achieves high agreement with human evaluators while being significantly cheaper and faster.

The paper demonstrates that SAFE-based evaluation correlates well with human judgment for long-form factual accuracy. The approach is applicable to survey generation factuality evaluation, though the web-search verification approach may need adaptation for scientific claim verification from paper-specific corpora.
