## Method Description

SGSimEval proposes a similarity-enhanced evaluation framework that combines LLM-based scoring with quantitative text similarity metrics for automated survey evaluation. The approach addresses the limitations of pure LLM-as-a-Judge (which can be biased by fluency) and pure automatic metrics (which miss semantic quality).

The framework computes multiple similarity dimensions between generated and reference surveys: lexical similarity (ROUGE), semantic similarity (embedding cosine), structural similarity (outline tree matching), and citation similarity (overlap and positioning). These are combined with LLM-based quality ratings through a calibration model that weights each component based on its correlation with human preferences.

SGSimEval also contributes a set of human preference judgments for generated surveys, enabling calibration and validation of the combined metric. The approach is shown to correlate better with human judgments than either pure automatic metrics or pure LLM-as-a-Judge.

## Limitations

- Requires reference surveys for similarity computation — cannot evaluate without gold standard
- Component weighting calibrated on specific domain — may not generalize
- Citation similarity metric assumes reference citations are gold standard
