## Method Description

SurveyLens is the first discipline-aware benchmark for Automatic Survey Generation (ASG), comprising 1,000 surveys across 10 academic disciplines. The benchmark introduces a "dual-lens" evaluation approach: (1) general quality lens — assessing readability, coherence, and factual accuracy using standard metrics, and (2) discipline-specific lens — assessing domain-appropriate methodology, literature coverage, and citation practices specific to each field.

The dual-lens approach addresses a key limitation of prior ASG benchmarks that evaluate all surveys with the same criteria, ignoring that different disciplines have different standards for what constitutes a good survey. For example, computer science surveys value comprehensiveness and taxonomy, while humanities surveys value argumentation and synthesis of contrasting views. The benchmark includes human expert annotations for both lenses.

## Limitations

- 10 disciplines may not cover all academic fields
- Dual-lens evaluation requires domain expert annotators for each field
- Benchmark quality depends on human survey quality and annotation
- Does not provide specific system comparisons in available metadata
