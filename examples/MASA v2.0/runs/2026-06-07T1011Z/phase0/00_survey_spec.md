# SurveySpec

- **run_dir**: `.`
- **topic**: automated literature survey agents with citation graph expansion
- **reader_need**: A researcher or engineer building automated survey systems who needs to understand how LLM-based survey agents can leverage citation graph traversal and expansion for improved paper discovery and survey coverage.
- **scope_include**: LLM-based survey agents, citation graph expansion and traversal, automated paper discovery and retrieval, literature mapping tools, survey generation pipelines, reference chaining strategies, evaluation of survey quality. Time range: 2020–present.
- **scope_exclude**: Manual survey methodology without automation, traditional citation analysis (pure bibliometrics without LLM integration), generic RAG systems not targeting survey writing, recommender systems for non-survey use cases.
- **anchor_questions**:
  - Primary: How do current automated survey agents leverage citation graph expansion to improve coverage, relevance, and quality?
  - Secondary: What architectures, retrieval strategies, and evaluation protocols exist for LLM-driven literature survey systems that incorporate citation traversal?
- **concept_seed**: automated survey agent, citation graph expansion, literature review automation, survey generation, paper discovery, citation traversal, forward/backward reference chaining, LLM-based survey, academic paper retrieval, survey writing systems, citation-aware retrieval
- **expected_dimensions**: method, benchmark, metric, limitation, application, theory
- **quality_bar**: The final brief must be able to answer: (1) what citation expansion strategies currently exist and how they integrate with LLM survey agents, (2) what evaluations have been conducted (benchmarks, metrics, datasets), (3) what open challenges remain in citation-aware automated survey generation.
