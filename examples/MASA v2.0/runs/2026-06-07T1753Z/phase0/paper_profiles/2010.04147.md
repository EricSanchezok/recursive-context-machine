## Method Description

This early work on automatic survey generation uses co-citation graph analysis combined with BERT extractive summarization. The pipeline: (1) computes a co-citation graph for papers in a topic, (2) identifies the most central/cited papers, (3) extracts key sentences from these papers using BERT-based extractive summarization, and (4) organizes extracted sentences into a coherent review structure.

The co-citation graph identifies which papers are most influential and how they relate to each other — papers that are frequently cited together are clustered. The extractive summarization selects the most informative sentences about each paper's contribution, methodology, and findings. This pre-LLM approach demonstrates that citation graph analysis can guide survey content selection even without generative models.

## Limitations

- Extractive only — does not generate new text or synthesis
- Co-citation analysis limits coverage to well-cited papers
- BERT extractive may miss nuanced relationships between papers
- Pre-dates modern LLM capabilities for abstractive summarization
