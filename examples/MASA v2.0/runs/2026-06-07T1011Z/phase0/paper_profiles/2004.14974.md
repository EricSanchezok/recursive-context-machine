## Description

SciFact is a dataset for scientific claim verification, where systems must determine whether a claim is supported or refuted by a given evidence corpus. It contains 1,409 expert-annotated claim-evidence pairs from biomedical literature, with labels of SUPPORT, REFUTE, or NOT ENOUGH INFO.

The task requires: (1) Retrieving relevant evidence for a claim from a paper corpus; (2) Determining the relationship between the claim and evidence (support/refute/neutral). SciFact established the scientific claim verification task that is directly relevant to survey generation — verifying that claims in generated surveys are supported by cited papers.

SciFact has become a standard evaluation task for scientific NLI and claim verification. Its methodology (claim decomposition, evidence retrieval, relation classification) directly informs citation verification in survey generation.
