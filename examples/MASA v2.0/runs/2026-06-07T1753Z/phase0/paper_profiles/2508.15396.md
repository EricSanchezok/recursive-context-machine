## Method Description

This paper provides a unified taxonomy of attribution, citation, and quotation in evidence-based text generation, surveying 134 papers and 300 metrics across 7 dimensions. The taxonomy covers: (1) source types (papers, passages, authors) and what gets cited, (2) citation granularity (paper-level, passage-level, claim-level), (3) attribution mechanisms (inline citations, footnote references, parenthetical), (4) faithfulness dimensions, (5) evaluation metrics, (6) generation strategies, and (7) application domains.

The survey provides a comprehensive framework for understanding and evaluating citation practices in LLM-generated content, directly relevant to designing citation-aware survey generation systems. The 300+ metrics catalog provides a resource for selecting appropriate evaluation approaches.
