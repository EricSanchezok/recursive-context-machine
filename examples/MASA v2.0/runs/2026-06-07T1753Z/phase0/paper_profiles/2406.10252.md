## Method Description

AutoSurvey is a seed paper for automated survey generation that introduces a structured pipeline with outline drafting, section generation, and refinement stages. The pipeline: (1) Outline — an LLM generates a structured outline for the survey based on the topic, identifying key sections and subsections, (2) Retrieval — for each section, relevant papers are retrieved via embedding search, (3) Drafting — each section is written based on the retrieved papers, with citations, and (4) Refinement — the full survey is reviewed and refined for coherence, coverage, and accuracy.

The structured outline provides a planning skeleton that guides retrieval and generation, ensuring comprehensive coverage of the topic. Each section is written with explicit citation to supporting papers, providing attribution for claims. The refinement stage checks for gaps, redundancies, and inconsistencies.

## Category Rationale

AutoSurvey operates as a single LLM pipeline with sequential stages (outline, retrieval, drafting, refinement). Despite multiple stages, a single LLM orchestrates all steps without specialized multi-agent coordination, placing it in single_agent_pipeline.

## Limitations

- No citation graph traversal — relies entirely on embedding retrieval
- Single-pass generation without iterative improvement
- Outline quality depends on LLM's understanding of the topic
- May miss papers that use different terminology for the same concepts
