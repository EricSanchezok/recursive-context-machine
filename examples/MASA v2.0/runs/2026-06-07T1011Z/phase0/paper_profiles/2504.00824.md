## Method Description

ScholarCopilot introduces a dynamic retrieval-token-gated architecture that jointly trains citation generation and academic writing. Rather than treating retrieval and generation as separate stages, ScholarCopilot embeds retrieval tokens directly into the LLM's generation process — at each generation step, the model can decide to retrieve a paper and cite it inline.

The system fine-tunes Qwen-2.5-7B on a dataset of 500K arXiv papers, training the model to generate text with interleaved citations. A retrieval token (<RET>) is inserted into the training data at positions where a citation would naturally occur. During inference, when the model generates a <RET> token, it triggers a retrieval from a pre-indexed paper database, and the retrieved paper ID is inserted as the citation. This gating mechanism ensures that citations are generated at contextually appropriate positions.

The trained model produces academic text with citations that are grounded in retrieved papers, eliminating the common problem of hallucinated citations. The approach is trained on the full arXiv corpus, giving it broad domain coverage, and achieves significantly higher citation accuracy than prompt-based methods without fine-tuning.

## Key Innovations

- Retrieval-token-gated architecture enabling learned citation placement decisions
- Fine-tuned specialist model (Qwen-2.5-7B) trained on 500K arXiv papers
- End-to-end training for joint academic writing and citation generation

## Method Category

single_agent_pipeline

## Category Rationale

ScholarCopilot uses a single fine-tuned LLM with an integrated retrieval mechanism. The architecture is single-agent — the model handles both generation and retrieval decisions internally through learned token patterns. There is no multi-agent coordination or iterative refinement.

## Limitations

- No citation graph awareness — retrieval is from a flat corpus without graph traversal
- Requires fine-tuning — cannot be applied to arbitrary LLMs without retraining
- Single-pass generation with no refinement loop
- Citation placement is learned from training data — may not generalize to unusual citation patterns
- 7B parameter model may be less capable than larger LLMs for complex survey writing
