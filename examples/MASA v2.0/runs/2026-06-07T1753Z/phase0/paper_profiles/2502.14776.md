## Method Description

SurveyX introduces an AttributeTree pre-processing approach for survey generation, combined with online retrieval and re-polishing. The AttributeTree is a structured representation of the survey topic that decomposes it into attributes (methods, datasets, benchmarks, applications, challenges) organized hierarchically. This structure guides retrieval — for each attribute node, relevant papers are retrieved — and generation — the survey is organized along the attribute hierarchy.

The pipeline consists of: (1) AttributeTree construction from the topic specification, (2) online retrieval of papers for each attribute, (3) section generation organized by attribute hierarchy, and (4) re-polishing for coherence and quality. The AttributeTree provides a more structured planning skeleton than a simple outline, ensuring comprehensive coverage of the topic's key dimensions.

## Category Rationale

SurveyX operates as a single LLM pipeline with the AttributeTree providing structured planning. Despite the sophisticated AttributeTree pre-processing, the system uses a single agent for all stages.

## Limitations

- No citation graph traversal for literature discovery
- AttributeTree construction quality depends on topic understanding
- Each attribute retrieved independently — may miss cross-attribute relationships
- Single-pass generation without iterative refinement
