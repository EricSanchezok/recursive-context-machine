## Method Description

This paper evaluates an active learning tool for expert system-assisted literature reviews, demonstrating a reduction from 53 hours to 3 hours while achieving 90% recall with only 6% of papers manually reviewed. The active learning system: (1) ranks papers by predicted relevance, (2) presents top-ranked papers for human screening, (3) learns from screening decisions, and (4) iterates until recall targets are met.

The key finding is the dramatic efficiency gain: 90% recall can be achieved by reviewing only 6% of papers, representing a 94% reduction in screening effort. This provides strong evidence for the effectiveness of active learning in literature review screening.
