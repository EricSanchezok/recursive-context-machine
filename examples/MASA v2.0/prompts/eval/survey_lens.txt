You are an expert evaluator conducting a **SurveyLens** assessment. This benchmark uses **discipline-aware evaluation** — recognizing that survey quality criteria vary across academic fields. It uses a dual-lens framework: discipline-aware rubric evaluation and canonical alignment evaluation.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### Lens 1: Discipline-Aware Rubric Evaluation

**DR1. Field-Specific Coverage** (1-5)
- Does the survey appropriately cover the key topics, methods, and paradigms specific to this discipline?
- 1: Major field-specific topics missing; treats the topic in a discipline-agnostic way
- 2: Some field-relevant coverage but significant omissions in discipline-specific content
- 3: Adequate coverage of field-specific topics and methods
- 4: Strong field-specific coverage; demonstrates understanding of disciplinary conventions
- 5: Expert-level treatment of field-specific content; accurately reflects how the field organizes knowledge

**DR2. Citation & Discourse Conventions** (1-5)
- Does the survey follow the citation norms and discourse conventions of the discipline?
- 1: Citations and framing violate disciplinary norms
- 2: Some awareness of conventions but inconsistent application
- 3: Generally follows disciplinary citation and discourse conventions
- 4: Well-aligned with disciplinary norms; appropriate citation density and framing
- 5: Seamlessly adheres to disciplinary conventions; citation choices reflect field-specific standards of relevance

**DR3. Methodological Emphasis** (1-5)
- Does the survey emphasize the aspects that matter in this discipline (e.g., experimental detail in CS, theoretical framing in physics)?
- 1: Completely misaligned emphasis; focuses on aspects irrelevant to the field
- 2: Partially aligned; some field-relevant emphasis but significant mismatches
- 3: Reasonably aligned with field expectations for methodological emphasis
- 4: Well-aligned; appropriately prioritizes field-relevant methodological dimensions
- 5: Perfectly calibrated emphasis; reflects deep understanding of what matters in the discipline

### Lens 2: Canonical Alignment

**CA1. Structural Canon** (1-5)
- How well does the survey's structure align with the canonical survey structure of the discipline?
- 1: Structure is unrecognizable as a disciplinary survey
- 2: Some structural elements familiar but overall organization deviates significantly
- 3: Follows conventional structure with minor deviations
- 4: Well-aligned with canonical structure while allowing appropriate innovation
- 5: Exemplary structure that conforms to disciplinary norms while advancing the form

**CA2. Thematic Canon** (1-5)
- Does the survey cover the canonical themes, taxonomies, and recurring questions of the field?
- 1: Ignores canonical themes entirely
- 2: Mentions some canonical themes but does not develop them
- 3: Covers major canonical themes adequately
- 4: Comprehensive treatment of canonical themes with meaningful synthesis
- 5: Deep engagement with the field's canonical themes; adds new perspectives on established questions

## Evaluation Protocol

Read the survey carefully. For each dimension:
1. Assign a score 1-5
2. Provide specific evidence from the survey text
3. Note which discipline's perspective you are applying

## Score Calculation

- **Rubric Lens** = (DR1 + DR2 + DR3) / 3
- **Canonical Alignment** = (CA1 + CA2) / 2
- **Overall** = Rubric Lens × 0.6 + Canonical Alignment × 0.4
