## Method Description

CueLearner proposes a method for bootstrapping and policy adaptation from relative feedback — learning from comparisons between trajectories (e.g., "this output is better than that one") rather than absolute ratings. The method uses relative feedback to initialize (bootstrap) a policy without requiring pre-existing data, then continuously adapts the policy as new comparisons become available.

The approach is particularly suited for settings where absolute quality assessment is difficult but comparative judgments are easy — a common scenario in survey evaluation (judges can compare two surveys for overall quality but struggle to assign absolute scores). The bootstrapping capability enables initial policy learning from minimal feedback.
