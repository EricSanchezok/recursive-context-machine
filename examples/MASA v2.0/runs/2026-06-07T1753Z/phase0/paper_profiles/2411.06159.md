## Method Description

This paper introduces a Mixture of Knowledge Minigraph Agents (KMCA) and Multi-Perspective Section Agents (MPSA) for literature review generation. The KMCA approach decomposes the literature into multiple "minigraphs" — subgraphs of the citation graph focused on specific aspects of the topic. Each minigraph agent specializes in one aspect, retrieving and synthesizing literature within its subgraph. The MPSA approach generates different sections of the survey from different perspectives, then integrates them.

The minigraph construction uses prompt-based graph extraction: an LLM identifies papers and citation relationships relevant to a specific subtopic, constructing a focused subgraph. Multiple minigraph agents work in parallel, each producing a subtopic analysis. The MPSA then coordinates the agents to produce a coherent multi-perspective survey.

## Category Rationale

This paper uses multiple specialized minigraph agents operating in parallel, coordinated by a Multi-Perspective Section Agent. The mixture-of-experts architecture with agent specialization qualifies it as a multi_agent_pipeline.

## Limitations

- Prompt-based minigraph construction may have variable quality
- Parallel agent execution adds computational cost
- MPSA integration may not fully capture cross-minigraph relationships
- Minigraph boundary definition depends on LLM's topic understanding
