## Method Description

This paper applies Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) to improve the correctness and alignment of automatically generated feedback. Rather than training a reward model separately, DPO directly optimizes the LLM policy to prefer feedback that is more accurate, helpful, and aligned with human preferences. The method uses pairs of human-annotated feedback examples (good vs. bad feedback) to fine-tune the model via preference optimization.

The approach is relevant to survey generation because survey quality depends critically on the correctness of paper summaries and citation attributes. RL-based optimization could be applied to improve the accuracy of paper descriptions, citation context, and comparative analysis in generated surveys — rewarding correct claims and penalizing hallucinated or inaccurate statements.
