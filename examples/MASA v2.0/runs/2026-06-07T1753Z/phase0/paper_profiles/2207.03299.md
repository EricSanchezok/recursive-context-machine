## Method Description

This paper demonstrates that citation clusters are complementary to query-based search for academic information retrieval. The study compares retrieval using citation clusters (papers grouped by their citation relationships) against standard query-based search (keyword matching, embedding similarity), finding that the two approaches retrieve different but complementary sets of relevant papers.

The implication for survey systems is that exclusive reliance on query-based search may miss relevant papers that are structurally related but lexically and semantically different. Citation clusters provide a different perspective on relevance — structural proximity rather than semantic similarity — and the best results come from combining both approaches.
