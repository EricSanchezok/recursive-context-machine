## Method Description

This paper proposes a knowledge graph approach for systematic literature review automation. The method constructs a knowledge graph from scientific papers, extracting entities (methods, datasets, findings, concepts) and their relationships (improves, uses, compares with). The knowledge graph then supports automated SLR stages: (1) screening — papers are classified for relevance using graph-based features, (2) data extraction — entity and relationship extraction populates structured data tables, and (3) synthesis — patterns and trends are identified from the knowledge graph.

The knowledge graph provides a structured representation of the literature that enables automated reasoning about relationships between papers, methods, and findings. The approach is designed to support the full SLR lifecycle rather than focusing on a single stage.
