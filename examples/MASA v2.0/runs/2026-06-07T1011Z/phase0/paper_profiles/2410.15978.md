## Method Description

PROMPTHEUS is a human-centered SLR pipeline that integrates LLM capabilities with established systematic review methodology (PRISMA guidelines). The pipeline is designed to support — not replace — human reviewers, providing AI assistance at each stage of the SLR process while maintaining human oversight and decision-making.

The pipeline includes: (1) Research Question Formulation — LLM helps refine and structure the research question; (2) Search Strategy — LLM generates search queries across multiple databases; (3) Screening — LLM assists with title/abstract screening using inclusion/exclusion criteria; (4) Data Extraction — LLM extracts structured data from included papers; (5) Synthesis — LLM generates narrative synthesis tables and draft findings; (6) Quality Assessment — LLM flags potential quality issues. At each stage, the human reviewer validates, corrects, or overrides the LLM's output.

PROMPTHEUS follows established SLR best practices (PRISMA-compliant reporting, dual-screening principles, pre-registration) while leveraging LLM efficiency gains. The system is evaluated on a case study in software engineering, demonstrating significant time savings while maintaining review quality.

## Method Category

## Category Rationale

PROMPTHEUS is fundamentally human-centered — the LLM assists but the human makes all final decisions at each stage. This hybrid human-AI interaction pattern, combined with adherence to PRISMA systematic review methodology, clearly places it in the hybrid_interactive category.

## Limitations

- High human effort compared to fully automated systems — requires domain expertise
- No citation graph awareness — follows traditional database search methodology
- Case study limited to software engineering — generalizability unclear
- Human validation requirement limits scalability to very large reviews
