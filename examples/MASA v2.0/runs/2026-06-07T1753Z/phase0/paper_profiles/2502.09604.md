## Method Description

SelfCite uses a context ablation reward for self-supervised alignment of citation attribution, achieving +5.3 F1 on LongBench-Cite. The method works by: (1) generating text with inline citations, (2) for each citation, "ablating" the cited context — removing the cited passage and measuring how much the generation quality drops, (3) using this quality drop as a reward signal to train the model to cite context that actually contributes to generation quality.

The context ablation measures whether the cited passage is genuinely informative for the generation. If removing the cited passage significantly reduces quality, the citation is well-attributed; if quality doesn't change, the citation may be spurious. The self-supervised approach requires only the source text, not human annotations.

## Limitations

- Requires access to cited source text for context ablation
- Training cost may be significant
- Context ablation may not capture all aspects of citation quality
- Focused on individual citation quality, not overall survey structure
