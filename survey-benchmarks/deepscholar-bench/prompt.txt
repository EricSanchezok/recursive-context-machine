## DeepScholar-Bench Evaluation Dimensions (From Paper)

### 1. Knowledge Synthesis (40% Weight)

**KS1. Organization & Coherency** (0-100)
- Measures how well the content is organized and how coherent the narrative is
- 0-20: Disorganized, incoherent, difficult to follow
- 21-40: Poorly organized with disjointed sections
- 41-60: Moderately organized with reasonable flow
- 61-80: Well-organized with clear structure and logical flow
- 81-100: Exceptionally well-organized with coherent narrative and smooth transitions

**KS2. Nugget Coverage** (0-100)
- Measures coverage of essential facts and key information
- Score = percentage of key factual nuggets from the reference that are covered
- 0-20: Missing most key facts; significant gaps in coverage
- 21-40: Covers some basic facts but missing important details
- 41-60: Covers essential facts with reasonable completeness
- 61-80: Comprehensive coverage of key facts and details
- 81-100: Exceptionally thorough coverage capturing all essential nuggets

### 2. Retrieval Quality (30% Weight)

**RQ1. Relevance Rate** (0-100)
- Measures average relevance among all referenced sources
- 0-20: Most references are irrelevant or tangential
- 21-40: Some relevant references but many are off-topic
- 41-60: Majority of references are relevant to the topic
- 61-80: High relevance rate; almost all references are pertinent
- 81-100: Excellent relevance; every reference directly supports the content

**RQ2. Document Importance** (0-100)
- Measures how notable/influential the referenced sources are
- Score = average normalized citation count or venue impact percentile
- 0-20: References mostly low-impact, obscure works
- 21-40: Mix of low and moderate impact works
- 41-60: Reasonable coverage of impactful works
- 61-80: Strong coverage of high-impact, influential papers
- 81-100: Excellent coverage of the field's most influential works

**RQ3. Reference Coverage** (0-100)
- Assesses whether the referenced set covers key, important references
- 0-20: Missing most key references in the field
- 21-40: Some key references included but significant omissions
- 41-60: Covers most important references
- 61-80: Comprehensive coverage of essential references
- 81-100: Complete coverage of all key references in the field

### 3. Verifiability (30% Weight)

**V1. Citation Precision** (0-100)
- Percentage of cited sources that actually support their accompanying claim
- Score = (supported claim-citation pairs / total verified pairs) 脳 100
- 0-20: Many citations are incorrect or do not support the claims
- 21-40: Some citations are correct but notable errors exist
- 41-60: Most citations correctly support their claims
- 61-80: High citation precision; claims consistently well-supported
- 81-100: Exceptional precision; every claim has appropriate supporting citation

**V2. Claim Coverage** (0-100)
- Percentage of claims that are fully supported by cited sources
- Score = (claims with citations / total claims) 脳 100
- 0-20: Most claims lack supporting citations
- 21-40: Some claims are supported but many are not
- 41-60: Majority of claims have supporting citations
- 61-80: High claim coverage; nearly all claims are cited
- 81-100: Complete claim coverage; every substantive claim is properly cited

## Evaluation Protocol

1. Read the generated "Related Work" section carefully
2. For each dimension, assign a score 0-100 with specific evidence
3. Quote relevant citations and claims to justify scores

## Score Calculation

Knowledge Synthesis = (KS1 + KS2) / 2
Retrieval Quality = (RQ1 + RQ2 + RQ3) / 3
Verifiability = (V1 + V2) / 2
Overall Score (Geometric Mean) = exp((ln(KS × 0.4 + RQ × 0.3 + V × 0.3)))
Overall = Knowledge Synthesis × 0.4 + Retrieval Quality × 0.3 + Verifiability × 0.3
