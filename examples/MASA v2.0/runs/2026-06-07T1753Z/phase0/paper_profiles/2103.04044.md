## Method Description

This paper provides a comprehensive survey of human-in-the-loop (HITL) approaches in NLP, covering feedback types, human interaction models, and learning methods. It taxonomizes HITL approaches along several dimensions: (1) when humans provide input (pre-processing, in-processing, post-processing), (2) what type of feedback humans provide (labels, corrections, preferences, demonstrations), (3) how the system incorporates feedback (online learning, active learning, interactive learning), and (4) how human effort is measured and minimized.

The survey catalogs 50+ HITL NLP systems across tasks including text classification, machine translation, summarization, and dialogue, extracting design patterns and best practices. This is directly relevant to designing human-in-the-loop survey generation systems where humans provide feedback on coverage, citation accuracy, and readability.
