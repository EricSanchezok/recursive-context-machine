## Method Description

LitChat is a conversational literature exploration agent that constructs a knowledge graph and generates evidence-based insights through natural language dialogue. The system works by: (1) building a knowledge graph from retrieved papers, extracting entities (papers, authors, concepts, methods, datasets) and their relationships (citations, uses, extends, compares), (2) allowing users to explore the literature through conversational interaction, asking questions like "What are the main approaches in this field?" or "How does Method A compare to Method B?", and (3) generating evidence-based responses that cite specific papers and passages.

The knowledge graph construction combines NLP entity extraction with citation relationship extraction to build a structured representation of the literature. The conversational interface enables intuitive exploration without requiring users to learn complex query languages. Responses are grounded in the knowledge graph with explicit citations to source papers.

## Category Rationale

LitChat provides a conversational interface for human-led literature exploration, with a knowledge graph supporting evidence-based responses. The human drives the exploration through natural language, supported by AI-generated insights, making it hybrid_interactive.

## Limitations

- Conversational format may not produce structured survey outputs
- Knowledge graph quality depends on entity extraction accuracy
- Citation relationships may have gaps or errors
- Scalability to large literature corpora may affect response time
