## 3. Core Architectures — Single-Agent, Multi-Agent, and the Controlled Comparison Gap

This section dissects the three dominant architectural paradigms — single-agent pipelines, multi-agent systems, and iterative refinement — and systematically audits the evidence for each. The central finding is that the field's most important claim — that multi-agent architectures outperform single-agent pipelines — rests on a single controlled comparison. We examine each paradigm in turn, then quantify the evidence gap.
