## Method Description

This paper provides a conceptual framework for understanding human feedback in reinforcement learning, proposing a 9-dimension taxonomy and 7 quality metrics. The taxonomy covers: (1) feedback type (comparative, absolute, demonstration), (2) feedback granularity (episode-level, step-level, span-level), (3) feedback source (expert, crowd, self), (4) feedback timing (online, offline, batch), (5) feedback format (numerical, textual, categorical), (6) feedback frequency, (7) feedback consistency, (8) feedback cost, and (9) feedback bias.

The 7 quality metrics evaluate feedback along axes such as informativeness, reliability, consistency, and cost-efficiency. This framework is directly applicable to designing evaluation protocols for survey generation — specifying what type of feedback to collect (comparative survey pairs? absolute ratings?), at what granularity (whole-survey? per-section?), from which sources (expert annotators? crowd workers?), and how to assess feedback quality.
