You are an expert evaluator conducting a SurveyBench-style assessment of an automatically generated academic survey.

## SurveyBench Evaluation Dimensions

You must evaluate the survey along the following dimensions, each scored 1-5 with detailed justification.

### A. Outline Quality (权重 30%)

**A1. Coverage Breadth** (1-5)
- 1: Covers only a narrow subset of the field; major research directions missing
- 2: Covers some areas but has significant gaps in important sub-topics
- 3: Covers most major research directions with minor omissions
- 4: Broad coverage of the field; all key sub-areas are represented
- 5: Exceptionally comprehensive; covers the full landscape including emerging directions

**A2. Logical Coherence** (1-5)
- 1: No clear organizational logic; sections seem randomly ordered
- 2: Weak logical flow; transitions between topics are abrupt or unclear
- 3: Generally coherent structure; most sections follow logically from previous ones
- 4: Well-organized with clear progression; categories are meaningful and non-overlapping
- 5: Elegant structure that builds understanding progressively; taxonomy is insightful and natural

### B. Content Quality (权重 50%)

**B1. Synthesis Granularity** (1-5)
- 1: Mere listing of papers without synthesis; no comparison or grouping
- 2: Basic grouping of papers but comparisons are superficial
- 3: Meaningful synthesis with reasonable comparison of approaches across papers
- 4: Detailed synthesis with nuanced comparisons; strengths/weaknesses clearly articulated
- 5: Expert-level synthesis identifying subtle distinctions, trade-offs, and evolution of ideas

**B2. Clarity of Insights** (1-5)
- 1: No original insights; purely descriptive
- 2: Few insights; mostly paraphrasing individual paper contributions
- 3: Some cross-cutting insights and observations beyond individual papers
- 4: Multiple original insights identifying trends, gaps, and tensions in the field
- 5: Deep, novel insights that would be valuable even to domain experts; identifies blind spots and unexamined assumptions

### C. Non-textual Richness & Reference Quality (权重 20%)

**C1. Reference Relevance & Coverage** (1-5)
- 1: References are sparse or mostly irrelevant
- 2: Some relevant references but key works are missing
- 3: Adequate reference coverage; most important works cited
- 4: Comprehensive reference set covering seminal and recent works
- 5: Expert-curated reference list; appropriate balance of seminal, recent, and niche contributions

**C2. Non-textual Elements** (1-5)
- 1: No figures, tables, or structured comparisons
- 2: Minimal use of tables or figures; mostly text
- 3: Some comparative tables or figures that aid understanding
- 4: Well-designed tables and figures that effectively summarize comparisons
- 5: Excellent use of taxonomies, comparison tables, and diagrams that substantially enhance comprehension

## Evaluation Protocol

Read the survey carefully. For each dimension:
1. Assign a score 1-5
2. Provide specific evidence from the survey text to justify the score
3. Quote the relevant section headers or passages

After scoring all dimensions, compute:
- **Weighted Total** = A1×0.15 + A2×0.15 + B1×0.25 + B2×0.25 + C1×0.10 + C2×0.10

Then provide:
- **Assessment summary** (2-3 sentences)
- **Strengths** (top 3)
- **Weaknesses/Gaps** (top 3)
- **Recommendations for improvement**
