## Description

This paper presents a large-scale multi-agent system with 30 specialized agents for planning and control of complex research tasks, including literature survey generation. The agents span roles such as Searcher, Analyst, Writer, Reviewer, FactChecker, Methodologist, DomainExpert, and numerous sub-specialties.

The architecture uses a hierarchical control structure: a high-level Planner Agent decomposes tasks into sub-tasks; mid-level Controller Agents manage groups of specialist agents; and specialist agents execute specific functions. Communication follows a publish-subscribe pattern where agents subscribe to task queues matching their capability profile.

The system is notable for its scale (30 agents) and explicit modeling of agent capability profiles for task assignment. However, the paper focuses more on the planning and control architecture than on survey-specific generation quality, positioning it as a general-purpose research assistant framework.
