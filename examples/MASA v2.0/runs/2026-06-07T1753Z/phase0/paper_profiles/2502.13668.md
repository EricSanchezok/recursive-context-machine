## Method Description

PeerQA is a scientific QA dataset derived from peer reviews, consisting of 579 question-answer pairs from 208 papers. The dataset includes both evidence retrieval — answers must cite specific passages from papers — and unanswerable questions — questions that cannot be answered from the paper alone, requiring the system to recognize knowledge boundaries.

The unanswerable questions are a key feature: they test whether systems can recognize when a question cannot be answered from available evidence, rather than hallucinating an answer. This is particularly relevant for survey generation, where systems must accurately represent gaps and uncertainties in the literature.
