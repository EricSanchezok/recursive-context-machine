## Method Description

Scholastic is a machine-in-the-loop system for inductive and interpretive text analysis with interactive visualization. The system supports: (1) clustering — an AI model clusters papers by content similarity, (2) visualization — the clusters are displayed in an interactive visual interface, (3) human interpretation — the human examines clusters, labels themes, and adjusts cluster assignments, and (4) learning — the AI incorporates human adjustments to improve its clustering model.

The approach is "machine-in-the-loop" rather than "human-in-the-loop": the AI does the heavy lifting (clustering, similarity computation) while the human provides high-level interpretation and quality control. The interactive visualization enables the human to understand and guide the AI's analysis. This is relevant to survey generation where a human might guide literature clustering and thematic organization.

## Category Rationale

Scholastic uses a machine-in-the-loop design where the AI performs analysis and the human provides high-level guidance through interactive visualization. The human is integral to the process, making it hybrid_interactive.

## Limitations

- Focused on text analysis (clustering), not survey generation
- No citation graph or retrieval capabilities
- Requires human effort for cluster interpretation and adjustment
- Scalability to large literature corpora may be limited by visualization space
