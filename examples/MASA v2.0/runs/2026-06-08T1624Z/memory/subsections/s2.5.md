### 2.5 Phase 4: Evaluation Maturation (2025–2026)

The recognition that cross-system comparison was impossible drove the rapid emergence of dedicated ASG benchmarks. **SurveyBench** [arXiv:2510.03120] is the most comprehensive: a quiz-driven evaluation using 11,343 arXiv topics and 4,947 human surveys, measuring whether generated surveys contain accurate factual knowledge via QA accuracy. **SurveyEval** [arXiv:2512.02763] evaluates across 7 subjects on three dimensions: overall quality, outline coherence, and reference accuracy. **SurGE** [arXiv:2508.15658] provides the largest evaluation corpus (1M papers) across four dimensions: coverage, accuracy, structure, and readability. **SurveyLens** [arXiv:2602.11238] introduces discipline-aware evaluation with 1,000 human surveys across 10 disciplines, recognizing that survey quality standards vary by field. **DeepSurvey-Bench** [arXiv:2601.15307] focuses on academic value — information value and scholarly communication quality — moving beyond surface metrics toward the substance that distinguishes excellent surveys from competent summaries.

The irony of Phase 4 is acute: these benchmarks were designed to solve the evaluation comparability crisis, yet no Phase 1–3 system has been evaluated on any of them. The field now has multiple standards for evaluation — SurveyBench, SurveyEval, SurGE, SurveyLens, and SGSimEval [arXiv:2508.11310] — which means it effectively still has none. Every system still reports on custom metrics, and the benchmarks exist in parallel without community convergence on a shared protocol — a pattern that echoes the metric dispersion of earlier phases.
