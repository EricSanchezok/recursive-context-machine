## Method Description

LitSearch is a retrieval benchmark for scientific literature consisting of 597 ML/NLP queries. The benchmark reveals a 24.8% gap between BM25 and dense retrievers, indicating significant room for improvement in scientific literature retrieval. The queries are designed to test different retrieval challenges: finding papers by methodology, by dataset, by task, and by contribution type.

The benchmark includes both query-paper relevance judgments and query-passage relevance judgments, enabling evaluation at both the paper and passage level. The 24.8% gap between sparse (BM25) and dense retrieval methods highlights the need for better retrieval approaches in the scientific domain, where terminology variation and concept density pose unique challenges.

## Limitations

- ML/NLP focused — may not generalize to all scientific domains
- 597 queries may not cover all retrieval challenges
- Relevance judgments may have annotator bias
- Does not address survey generation or citation-aware retrieval
