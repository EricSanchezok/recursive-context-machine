## Method Description

TOBY is an interactive visualization tool designed to help researchers explore and understand data presented in academic survey papers. The tool integrates multiple views: (1) a hierarchical taxonomy view that organizes survey content into topics and subtopics, (2) a document similarity view that shows how papers cluster by content, and (3) a citation network view that visualizes the relationships between papers through citation links.

TOBY processes survey papers and their cited references to construct a multi-faceted exploration environment. The hierarchical taxonomy is extracted from the survey's own structure (section headings, subsections, and topic groupings), while the citation network is built from the survey's reference list. Document similarity is computed using textual embeddings of paper abstracts or full text. The tool allows users to navigate between these views, clicking on a topic in the taxonomy to highlight relevant papers in the citation network, or selecting a paper cluster to see its topic coverage.

## Category Rationale

TOBY is a visualization and exploration tool that presents citation networks alongside taxonomy and similarity views. It enhances retrieval with interactive graph-based exploration but does not generate surveys or use LLM agents. The citation network is one of three linked views, making it a graph_enhanced_retrieval method.

## Limitations

- Depends on the structure of existing survey papers; cannot generate new surveys
- Limited to the citation and content available within the source survey paper
- Visualization-only; no automated analysis or synthesis capabilities
- Scalability to large literature collections not clearly addressed
