## Method Description

This paper conducts a replicability study of neural network approaches for automating citation screening in systematic literature reviews. Testing on 23 standard SLR datasets, the study finds that simpler embedding models (e.g., word embeddings combined with logistic regression) outperform more complex deep learning models on 18 of 23 datasets.

The findings challenge the assumption that more complex models are always better for screening, suggesting that the simpler models generalize better across different review topics and are more robust to dataset-specific noise. The paper provides methodological guidance for selecting screening classifiers based on dataset characteristics.
