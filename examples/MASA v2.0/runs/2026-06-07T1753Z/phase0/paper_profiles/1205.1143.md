## Method Description

This paper proposes direction-aware citation analysis algorithms for academic network recommendation. The key insight is that the direction of citation edges matters — citing a paper is different from being cited by it. The method develops algorithms that distinguish between forward citation influence (being cited indicates importance) and backward citation influence (citing indicates awareness) and combines them in a tunable manner.

The tunable parameter controls the balance between recency bias (favoring recent, actively-cited papers) and tradition bias (favoring older, foundational papers). This pre-LLM work provides foundational algorithms for citation-aware recommendation that are still relevant for designing the retrieval components of modern survey agents, particularly for controlling temporal bias in citation expansion.
