## Method Description

This paper presents a comprehensive statistical survey of Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), covering reward modeling, Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), and Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback (RLAIF). It provides a unified statistical framework for understanding RLHF algorithms, analyzing their convergence properties, sample efficiency, and robustness to feedback quality.

The survey covers the theoretical foundations of preference-based optimization, including the Bradley-Terry model, Plackett-Luce model, and rank-based approaches. It analyzes how different feedback types (comparative, absolute, ranking) affect learning dynamics and final policy quality. This provides a theoretical foundation for applying RL-based optimization to survey generation — for example, optimizing survey policies based on human preferences for coverage, accuracy, and readability.
