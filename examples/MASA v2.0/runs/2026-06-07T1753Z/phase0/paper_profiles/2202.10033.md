## Method Description

This paper presents an open-source integrated framework for citation collection and screening using Bayesian active learning, achieving 95.6% efficiency with 100% sensitivity. The framework: (1) collects citations from multiple databases, (2) deduplicates and organizes them, (3) applies Bayesian active learning for screening prioritization, and (4) tracks screening progress with performance estimates.

The Bayesian active learning approach provides principled uncertainty estimates for screening decisions, enabling confident stopping when sufficient relevant papers have been identified. The 100% sensitivity (finding all relevant papers) with 95.6% efficiency (minimizing screening effort) demonstrates the framework's practical utility.
