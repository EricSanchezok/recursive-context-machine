## Description

This paper provides a comprehensive survey of citation generation and attribution in LLMs, covering methods, benchmarks, and open challenges. It systematically categorizes approaches to citation in LLM outputs: (1) Training-time methods where citation awareness is incorporated during model training; (2) Inference-time methods where citations are added post-hoc to generated text; (3) Retrieval-augmented methods where citations are generated inline with retrieval.

The survey identifies key challenges: citation accuracy (LLMs frequently cite papers that don't support their claims), citation completeness (LLMs miss relevant citations), and citation hallucination (LLMs invent plausible-sounding but non-existent citations). It also reviews benchmarks and evaluation metrics for citation quality.

As a survey paper, it provides a taxonomy and landscape overview that contextualizes individual method contributions. It is relevant as a reference work for understanding the citation generation landscape.
