## Method Description

This paper systematically evaluates ChatGPT's ability to screen articles for systematic reviews, comparing LLM-based screening against traditional ML classifiers. The study tests ChatGPT on multiple systematic review datasets, measuring sensitivity (ability to identify relevant papers), specificity (ability to exclude irrelevant papers), and consistency across repeated screenings.

The findings show that ChatGPT achieves competitive screening performance, with particular strengths in handling nuanced inclusion criteria that traditional classifiers find difficult. However, consistency across repeated screenings is lower than traditional ML approaches. The paper provides practical guidance for using LLMs in the screening stage of systematic reviews.
