## Method Description

This comprehensive monograph on RLHF covers instruction tuning, reward model training, rejection sampling, and policy optimization. It provides a practical guide to implementing RLHF pipelines, including dataset construction, reward model architecture design, and policy optimization strategies. The book covers both theoretical foundations and practical implementation details, including hyperparameter selection, training stability, and evaluation.

The monograph is relevant as a reference for applying RLHF to survey generation optimization. The rejection sampling approach — generating multiple survey candidates and selecting the best one based on a quality metric — is directly applicable. The reward modeling section provides guidance on designing quality metrics for survey evaluation.
