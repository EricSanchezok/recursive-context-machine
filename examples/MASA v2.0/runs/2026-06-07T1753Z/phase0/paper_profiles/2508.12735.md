## Method Description

This paper defines and distinguishes three phenomena in citation practices: (1) Citation accuracy — whether a citation correctly represents the cited paper's content, (2) Citation noise — citations that are topically or methodologically related but not directly relevant to the specific claim being made, and (3) Citation bias — systematic distortions in citation patterns due to prestige, recency, or social factors.

The paper provides CoARA (Coalition for Advancing Research Assessment) recommendations for mitigating these issues, including guidelines for citation evaluation in research assessment. This framework is directly relevant to survey generation: systems should aim for accurate citations, avoid citation noise (citing papers that are topically related but don't support the specific claim), and be aware of systematic biases.
