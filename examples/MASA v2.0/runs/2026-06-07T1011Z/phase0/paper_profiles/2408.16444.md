## Method Description

SurveySum is a benchmark dataset and evaluation framework for multi-article scientific summarization into survey sections. It provides 448 source paper sets with corresponding human-written survey sections, covering computer science and biomedical domains.

The dataset construction methodology: each source paper set contains 4-10 papers on a focused subtopic, paired with a human-written survey section that synthesizes these papers into coherent narrative prose with appropriate citations. The dataset is designed to evaluate the specific task of section-level survey writing (as opposed to full-survey generation or single-document summarization).

The paper also benchmarks several baseline methods on SurveySum, including extractive approaches, RAG-based methods, and full LLM prompting, providing performance baselines for future research. Evaluation uses ROUGE variants, citation recall/precision, and human evaluation dimensions.

## Limitations

- Limited to CS and biomedical domains
- Dataset size (448 sets) may limit statistical power for method comparison
