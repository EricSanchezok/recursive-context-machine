## Method Description

ProfOlaf is a semi-automated tool for systematic literature reviews that combines iterative snowballing with LLM-based analysis and human-in-the-loop filtering. The workflow is: (1) seed paper selection by the user, (2) iterative snowballing — forward and backward citation expansion from seeds, (3) LLM-based relevance assessment of retrieved papers, (4) human-in-the-loop filtering where the user validates LLM assessments, and (5) synthesis of the final paper set.

The semi-automated design means the human remains in control of key decisions (seed selection, filtering validation) while the LLM handles the labor-intensive work (running snowball iterations, assessing relevance). The iterative snowballing continues until coverage saturation or user-defined stopping criteria are met.

## Limitations

- Semi-automated — slower than fully automated approaches
- Snowballing quality depends on citation database coverage
