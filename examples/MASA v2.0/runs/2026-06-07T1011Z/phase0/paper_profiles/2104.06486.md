## Description

MS² (Medical Studies Summarization) is a dataset and benchmark for multi-document summarization of medical studies, containing 470K+ documents and 20K summaries. The focus is on synthesizing evidence from multiple medical studies, including handling contradictory evidence across studies.

The dataset: (1) 470K+ medical study abstracts and full texts; (2) 20K human-written summaries synthesizing findings across multiple studies; (3) Evidence contradictions — instances where studies report conflicting results that must be accurately represented in the summary; (4) Structured summarization targets including intervention, population, outcome, and findings.

MS² is relevant for survey generation because medical evidence synthesis requires handling contradictory findings — a challenge that general survey systems must also address. The contradiction-aware summarization component is particularly applicable.
