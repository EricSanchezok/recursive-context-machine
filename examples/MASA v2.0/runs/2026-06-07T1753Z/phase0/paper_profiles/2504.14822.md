## Method Description

InsightAgent is a human-centered multi-agent system for conducting systematic reviews that dramatically reduces the time from months to hours. The system integrates a human orchestrator with multiple specialized AI agents: (1) a Search agent that performs comprehensive literature search across databases, (2) a Screen agent that applies inclusion/exclusion criteria to filter papers, (3) an Extract agent that extracts relevant data from included papers, (4) a Synthesize agent that synthesizes findings across papers, and (5) a Quality agent that assesses risk of bias and study quality.

The human orchestrator defines the research question, specifies inclusion criteria, and validates intermediate results at each stage. The agents handle the labor-intensive work (searching, screening, extraction, synthesis) while the human provides oversight and makes judgment calls. The system reports a 27.2% quality improvement over manual systematic reviews while reducing completion time to 1.5 hours. The multi-agent architecture with human oversight is directly relevant to survey generation.

## Category Rationale

InsightAgent places the human at the center of the process, orchestrating specialized AI agents while providing oversight and validation at each stage. The human-in-the-loop design with multi-agent AI assistance makes it a hybrid_interactive system.

## Limitations

- Requires active human participation throughout the review process
- Multi-agent architecture adds computational cost
- Quality improvement measured in systematic review tasks, not all survey types
- Human orchestrator bottleneck — system speed depends on human response time
