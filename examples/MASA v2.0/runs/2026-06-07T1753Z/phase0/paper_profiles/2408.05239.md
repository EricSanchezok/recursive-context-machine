## Method Description

LRN (Literature Review Network) is the first explainable AI system explicitly designed to be PRISMA 2020-compliant for conducting systematic literature reviews (SLRs). The system implements the full PRISMA workflow: (1) Identification — searching databases and registers, (2) Screening — applying inclusion/exclusion criteria with explainable decisions, (3) Eligibility — full-text assessment with reasoning, and (4) Inclusion — final study selection with documented rationale.

The "explainable" aspect means that every screening decision is accompanied by a natural language explanation referencing specific criteria, making the process transparent and auditable. This PRISMA compliance is critical for systematic reviews that need to meet reporting standards. The system reports 84.78% accuracy and reduces review time from 11 months to 5 days.

## Category Rationale

LRN implements the PRISMA workflow as a sequential pipeline with explainable decisions at each stage. Despite having multiple stages, it operates as a single pipeline driven by one core model, placing it in single_agent_pipeline.

## Limitations

- Designed for systematic reviews (medical/health), not general survey generation
- PRISMA workflow may be overly rigid for some survey types
- Explainable decisions add computational overhead
- 84.78% accuracy may miss some relevant papers
