## Method Description

PaperQA2 is the successor to PaperQA, achieving superhuman synthesis performance on the LitQA2 benchmark. The system extends PaperQA with: (1) improved retrieval incorporating RAG with structured citation extraction, (2) contradiction detection — identifying and resolving conflicting claims across different papers, (3) improved citation chaining with breadth-aware expansion, and (4) multi-perspective synthesis that weighs evidence from multiple papers.

The contradiction detection component is a key innovation: it identifies when different papers make conflicting claims about the same topic and either resolves the contradiction through additional retrieval or presents both sides with supporting evidence. The system achieves performance exceeding human experts on literature-based question answering, demonstrating the potential of automated synthesis.

## Category Rationale

PaperQA2 extends the single-agent PaperQA architecture with improved retrieval, contradiction detection, and multi-perspective synthesis. It remains a single-agent pipeline where one LLM handles all stages.

## Limitations

- Focused on QA, not comprehensive survey generation
- Contradiction detection may miss subtle disagreements
- Breadth-aware chaining may increase computational cost
- Superhuman performance on benchmarks may not translate to all domains
