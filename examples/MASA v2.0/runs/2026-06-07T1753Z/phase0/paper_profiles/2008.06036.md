## Method Description

This paper studies reinforcement learning with weak feedback signals — specifically, trajectory-level scores rather than per-action rewards. The theoretical framework analyzes how agents can learn from feedback that is only provided at the end of an episode (e.g., "this survey is good/bad overall") rather than for each individual action (e.g., "this paragraph is well-written, this citation is accurate").

The paper provides convergence guarantees and sample complexity bounds for learning from trajectory-level feedback, along with algorithms for credit assignment — determining which actions in the trajectory contributed to the outcome. This is relevant to survey generation where quality feedback is typically global (whole-survey evaluations) rather than per-section or per-sentence.
