## Method Description

MARE (Multi-Agents collaboration framework for Requirements Engineering) is a role-based multi-agent framework with 5 specialized agents and 9 types of actions operating in a shared workspace. The agents include: (1) Manager — coordinates the process and assigns tasks, (2) Analyst — extracts and analyzes requirements, (3) Validator — checks requirements for consistency and completeness, (4) Negotiator — resolves conflicts between requirements, and (5) Documenter — produces structured requirements documentation.

While designed for requirements engineering, the role structure is directly transferable to survey generation: a Manager coordinates the survey writing process, an Analyst reviews retrieved literature, a Validator checks citation accuracy and coverage, a Negotiator resolves conflicting findings, and a Documenter produces the final survey. The shared workspace enables agents to access and modify a common artifact, similar to how survey agents might collaborate on a shared document.
