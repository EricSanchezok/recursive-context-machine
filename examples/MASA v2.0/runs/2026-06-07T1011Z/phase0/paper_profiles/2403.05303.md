## Description

ACLSum is a dataset for aspect-based summarization of scientific publications, where summaries are generated for specific aspects: motivation, method, result, contribution, and limitation. Each paper has separate summaries for each aspect, enabling fine-grained evaluation of aspect-specific generation.

The dataset includes: (1) 3,040 ACL anthology papers with aspect-specific summaries; (2) Five aspect dimensions: motivation, method, result, contribution, limitation; (3) Hierarchical evaluation covering both aspect-specific quality and overall coherence when aspects are combined.

ACLSum is relevant for survey generation because surveys often organize content by aspects (comparing methods, results, limitations across papers). Aspect-based summarization provides granular training and evaluation data.
