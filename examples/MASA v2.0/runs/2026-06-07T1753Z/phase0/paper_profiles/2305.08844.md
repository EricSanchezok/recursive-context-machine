## Method Description

RL4F (Reinforcement Learning for Feedback) presents a multi-agent architecture where an RL-trained critique generator produces natural language feedback to repair model outputs. The architecture consists of: (1) a Generator model that produces initial outputs, (2) a Critique generator (RL-trained) that produces feedback identifying errors and suggesting improvements, and (3) an iterative refinement loop where the Generator revises its output based on the Critique.

The Critique generator is trained via reinforcement learning: a reward model evaluates the quality of feedback (does it help the Generator improve?), and the Critique generator is optimized to produce feedback that maximizes downstream improvement. This approach is directly relevant to survey generation — a Critique agent could be RL-trained to identify coverage gaps, citation errors, and structural issues in generated surveys, providing targeted feedback for revision.

## Category Rationale

## Limitations

- Focused on general NLG output repair, not survey-specific quality issues
- RL training of critique generator requires downstream task evaluation
- Two-agent loop may converge slowly or oscillate
- Does not address literature retrieval or citation accuracy
