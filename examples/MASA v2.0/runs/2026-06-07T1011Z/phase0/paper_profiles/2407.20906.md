## Method Description

This paper presents a domain-specific automated review generation system for the PDH (propane dehydrogenation) catalysis domain. It combines domain-specific knowledge bases with LLM-based generation to produce structured scientific reviews in a specialized technical field.

The system uses: (1) A domain-specific paper corpus curated from catalysis journals; (2) Structured data extraction focusing on catalysts, conditions, and performance metrics; (3) Template-based review generation that fills structured sections (catalyst types, reaction conditions, performance comparison, mechanistic insights) using extracted data; (4) LLM-based narrative generation connecting the structured findings into coherent prose.

The domain-specific approach enables more precise evaluation than general-domain survey systems, as the review has well-defined comparison dimensions (e.g., conversion rate, selectivity, stability). The template structure ensures comprehensive coverage of the domain's key aspects.
