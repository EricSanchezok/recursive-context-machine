## Method Description

LitFM (Literature Foundation Model) is a structure-aware foundation model for citation graphs that achieves +28.1% precision on retrieval tasks. The model is pretrained on large-scale citation graph data using objectives that capture both semantic content (paper text) and structural context (citation neighborhood position). The architecture uses a graph transformer that processes attention over both text tokens and citation graph neighbors.

LitFM can be fine-tuned for downstream tasks including paper retrieval, citation recommendation, and literature search. The structure-aware pretraining enables the model to understand a paper's role in the citation network — not just what it says, but where it sits in the research landscape. The +28.1% precision improvement over text-only models demonstrates the value of incorporating citation graph structure.

## Limitations

- Large-scale pretraining may not be feasible for all research groups
- Graph transformer attention over citation neighbors may be computationally expensive
- Fine-tuning required for optimal downstream task performance
- Citation graph coverage affects representation quality for low-resource areas
