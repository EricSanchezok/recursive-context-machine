## Description

This paper introduces a multi-agent system that mimics a personalized research group for scientific literature review. The system assigns different agent roles corresponding to research group members (e.g., Research Director, Senior Researcher, Junior Researcher, Critical Reviewer), each with distinct expertise, reviewing focus, and interaction patterns.

The research group simulation works as follows: the Research Director agent formulates the review scope and assigns sub-topics to Senior Researcher agents; each Senior Researcher leads a investigation with Junior Researchers who retrieve and summarize papers; the Critical Reviewer agent scrutinizes findings for gaps, biases, and contradictions; the entire group then collaborates on producing a synthesized review. Agents communicate using structured messages that mirror academic discussion patterns.

The system is designed to be personalized — users can configure the "research group" composition, expertise distribution, and interaction dynamics. This flexibility allows the system to adapt to different review styles and requirements.
