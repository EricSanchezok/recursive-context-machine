## Method Description

This paper combines Research Knowledge Graphs (RKGs) with citation networks for citation recommendation, achieving +0.8 MAP over baselines. The RKG captures entity-level relationships (papers, methods, datasets, tasks) while the citation network captures paper-level relationships. The combined approach uses a joint embedding space where both entity relationships and citation relationships contribute to paper similarity.

The key insight is that citation recommendation benefits from both fine-grained entity-level knowledge (this paper uses specific methods/datasets) and coarse-grained paper-level knowledge (this paper is related to other papers). The combination captures complementary signals for relevance assessment.

## Limitations

- Requires construction of research knowledge graph, which is costly
- Knowledge graph coverage may be limited for some domains
- Joint embedding may not capture all relevant relationships
- Does not address survey generation or multi-hop traversal
