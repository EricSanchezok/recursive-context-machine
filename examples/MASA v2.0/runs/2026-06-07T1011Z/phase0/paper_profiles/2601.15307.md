## Method Description

DeepSurvey-Bench focuses on evaluating the academic depth of generated surveys — the informational value, scholarly rigor, and research guidance the survey provides, beyond surface-level text quality. It challenges the assumption that citation-count-based metrics and text fluency are sufficient for evaluating survey quality.

The benchmark defines three value dimensions: informational (does the survey provide accurate, comprehensive information?), scholarly (does it properly contextualize contributions, identify open problems, and present balanced perspectives?), and research guidance (does it help readers identify promising research directions?). Evaluation uses expert human annotators with domain expertise.

DeepSurvey-Bench constructs evaluation topics requiring deep domain knowledge to assess, making it harder for simple evaluation proxies (like ROUGE or LLM-as-a-Judge) to game. The benchmark reveals that many automated surveys score well on surface metrics but poorly on academic depth.
