You are an expert evaluator conducting a **SGSimEval** assessment. This benchmark combines LLM-based scoring with quantitative similarity metrics to provide a multifaceted evaluation of survey quality across outline, content, and references.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### E1. Outline Quality (1-5)
- Evaluate the structure, organization, and logical flow of the survey's outline
- 1: No clear structure; disjointed organization
- 2: Basic structure but lacks coherence or balance
- 3: Well-structured with clear section hierarchy and logical flow
- 4: Excellent structure with balanced coverage and thoughtful progression
- 5: Exceptional outline that demonstrates deep structural insight and novel organization

### E2. Content Quality (1-5)
- Evaluate the depth, accuracy, and synthesis quality of the survey content
- 1: Superficial, inaccurate, or purely descriptive
- 2: Basic synthesis with limited analysis or comparison
- 3: Meaningful synthesis, accurate content, some comparative analysis
- 4: Deep synthesis with nuanced comparisons; identifies trends and patterns
- 5: Expert-level synthesis with original insights critical evaluation, and comprehensive analysis

### E3. Reference Quality (1-5)
- Evaluate the relevance, coverage, and appropriateness of citations
- 1: Irrelevant or minimal references
- 2: Some relevant references but poor coverage or balance
- 3: Adequate references covering most key works
- 4: Comprehensive, well-balanced reference set
- 5: Expert-curated references; perfect balance of seminal, contemporary, and niche contributions

## Similarity-Enhanced Calibration

After scoring each dimension, produce a calibrated score that takes into account similarity to a high-quality human survey:
- **Lexical similarity** (ROUGE-based): how much terminology overlaps with expected survey content
- **Semantic similarity** (embedding-based): how well concepts align with expected coverage
- **Structural similarity**: how well the outline matches expected organization

For each dimension, note whether the survey demonstrates:
- **Human-comparable** (no significant gap from human quality)
- **Needs improvement** (noticeable gap)
- **Significant gap** (major deficiency)

## Evaluation Protocol

Read the survey carefully. For each dimension:
1. Assign a base score 1-5
2. Provide specific evidence
3. Note any similarity/alignment issues

## Score Calculation

- **Weighted Total** = E1 × 0.25 + E2 × 0.50 + E3 × 0.25

Content quality receives the highest weight as it is the core of survey utility.
