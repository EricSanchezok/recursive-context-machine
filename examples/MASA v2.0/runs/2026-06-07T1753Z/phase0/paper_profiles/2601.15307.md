## Method Description

DeepSurvey-Bench introduces a novel "academic value" evaluation dimension for automated survey generation, going beyond surface-level quality metrics (fluency, grammar) to assess whether a generated survey provides genuine academic utility. The benchmark evaluates: (1) whether the survey correctly identifies key papers and seminal works, (2) whether it accurately represents the state of research, (3) whether it identifies open problems and future directions, and (4) whether it provides a balanced perspective on controversial topics.

The benchmark includes a human-annotated dataset where domain experts have rated generated surveys on these academic value dimensions. This moves beyond n-gram overlap and embedding similarity to assess whether a survey would actually be useful to researchers — a much higher bar that captures whether the system has deeply understood the research landscape.

## Limitations

- Human annotation is expensive and expert-dependent
- "Academic value" is subjective and may vary across domains
- Dataset size may be limited by annotation cost
- Does not directly compare systems in available metadata
