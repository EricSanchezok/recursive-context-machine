## Method Description

This paper presents the early large-scale arXiv citation graph with 6.7M edges, documenting the construction of a citation graph from arXiv paper metadata. The paper discusses the characteristics of the arXiv citation graph: its power-law degree distribution, clustering coefficients, temporal patterns, and coverage limitations. It also analyzes citation patterns across arXiv subject categories.

The dataset and analysis provide foundational understanding of citation graph properties for researchers building citation-aware systems. The paper identifies key characteristics such as citation lag (time between paper publication and citing), citation decay (how citation rates change with age), and cross-category citation patterns.
