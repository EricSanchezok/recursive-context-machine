## Method Description

SurveyGen-I introduces coarse-to-fine retrieval with adaptive planning and a memory mechanism for survey generation. The system: (1) starts with coarse retrieval — broad topic-level search to identify relevant research areas, (2) adaptive planning — the plan evolves as more literature is discovered, (3) fine retrieval — targeted searches for each planned section, and (4) memory-guided writing — the system maintains a memory of discovered papers and their relationships to guide coherent narrative construction.

The coarse-to-fine approach ensures broad coverage before diving into specifics, preventing premature focus on narrow sub-areas. The adaptive planning means the outline can change as the system discovers literature that suggests different organizational structures. The memory mechanism tracks which papers have been covered and how they relate to each other.

## Limitations

- No citation graph traversal for structural literature discovery
- Adaptive planning may not always converge to optimal structure
- Memory mechanism may have limited capacity for large surveys
- Coarse-to-fine approach may miss papers in the "middle ground"
