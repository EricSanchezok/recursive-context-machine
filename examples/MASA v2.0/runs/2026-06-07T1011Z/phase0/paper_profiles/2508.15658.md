## Method Description

SurGE is a benchmark for automated survey generation focused on computer science. It provides a large-scale corpus of 1M+ papers and evaluates generated surveys across four dimensions: coverage (how well the survey covers the topic's key areas), referencing (citation accuracy and appropriateness), structure (organization and flow), and content (factual accuracy and clarity).

The benchmark uses Semantic Scholar's corpus as the paper database, providing a standardized retrieval environment for fair comparison across systems. Evaluation uses both automated metrics (citation F1, coverage scores) and LLM-based assessment using structured rubrics per dimension. SurGE is fully open-sourced, including the evaluation code, rubrics, and topic seeds.

SurGE's large paper corpus enables evaluation of retrieval systems at scale, testing whether survey systems can effectively discover relevant papers from a large pool. The four-dimension evaluation framework provides granular quality assessment beyond aggregate scoring.
