## rdc_read
Reads data from the shared Research Data Center (RDC).

Available entity_types:
- research: Current research project state (anchor, config, counters)
- ideas: All research ideas with statuses
- claims: Research claims with evidence links
- experiments: Experiment records with results
- lit_papers: Literature paper knowledge base
- lit_search: Search literature by query

Examples:
- To get the current research anchor: rdc_read(entity_type="research")
- To list selected ideas: rdc_read(entity_type="ideas", filter_status="selected")
- To search literature: rdc_read(entity_type="lit_search", search_query="attention mechanism")
