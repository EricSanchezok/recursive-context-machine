## Method Description

SGSimEval is a benchmark for evaluating Automatic Survey Generation (ASG) systems across three dimensions: (1) Outline quality — how well the survey structure matches human-written outlines, (2) Content quality — the accuracy, coverage, and coherence of the generated content, and (3) Reference quality — the correctness and relevance of cited references. The evaluation uses similarity-enhanced metrics that compare generated surveys against human-written reference surveys.

The benchmark provides a multi-faceted evaluation framework that captures different aspects of survey quality rather than a single aggregate score. The similarity enhancement uses both lexical similarity (n-gram overlap) and semantic similarity (embedding-based) to provide more nuanced comparisons. Human preference metrics are also incorporated to validate automated metrics against human judgments.

## Category Rationale

SGSimEval is an evaluation benchmark for ASG systems, not a system itself. It provides evaluation methodology but does not generate surveys.

## Limitations

- Benchmark quality depends on human-written reference surveys
- Similarity metrics may not capture all aspects of survey quality
- Three dimensions may not cover field-specific evaluation needs
- Does not provide specific system comparisons in available metadata
