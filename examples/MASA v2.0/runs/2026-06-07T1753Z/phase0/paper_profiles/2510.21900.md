## Method Description

IterSurvey introduces a recurrent outline generation approach with iterative refinement, evaluated on the Survey-Arena benchmark. The system generates multiple outline candidates, selects the best one, generates content for each section, and then iteratively refines based on self-evaluation. The key innovation is the recurrent outline generation — the outline is refined as content is generated, allowing the structure to adapt to discovered content.

The iterative workflow: (1) initial outline generation from topic, (2) section-by-section content generation with citations, (3) self-evaluation of each section, (4) outline revision based on discovered content gaps, and (5) content regeneration for revised sections. This iterative refinement loop continues until quality criteria are met.

## Limitations

- Iterative refinement may converge slowly for large surveys
- Self-evaluation quality depends on LLM capability
- No citation graph traversal for literature discovery
- Outline revision may not always converge to better structure
