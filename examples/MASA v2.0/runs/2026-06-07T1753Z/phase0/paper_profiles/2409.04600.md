## Method Description

This paper presents a meta-review of 3,788 articles on the use of LLMs for literature review, itself conducted using an LLM-automated systematic review methodology. It maps automation stages where LLMs are applied: (1) search and retrieval, (2) screening and filtering, (3) data extraction, (4) synthesis and summarization, and (5) quality assessment.

The meta-review compares GPT-based approaches against BERT-based approaches across these stages, finding that GPT models excel at synthesis and summarization while BERT-based models remain competitive for screening and classification tasks. The paper provides a systematic mapping of the LLM-for-review landscape, identifying gaps and opportunities.
