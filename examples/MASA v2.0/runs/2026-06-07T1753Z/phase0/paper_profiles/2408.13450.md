## Method Description

vitaLITy 2 is a RAG-based system for reviewing academic literature using LLMs, operating over a 66k-paper corpus with text embedding search. The system: (1) indexes a user-provided corpus of papers using text embeddings, (2) retrieves relevant papers for a given topic via embedding similarity, (3) extracts key findings and organizes them, and (4) generates a structured review with citations.

The system is designed for researchers to upload their own paper collections and get reviews of that specific literature, rather than searching global databases. This makes it suitable for targeted reviews of a known body of work.

## Category Rationale

vitaLITy 2 is a single RAG pipeline operating over a user-provided corpus. It is a single-agent system without multi-agent coordination.

## Limitations

- Limited to user-provided corpus (66k papers max in described setup)
- No citation graph traversal for literature discovery
- Relies entirely on embedding similarity for retrieval
- Single-pass generation without iterative refinement
