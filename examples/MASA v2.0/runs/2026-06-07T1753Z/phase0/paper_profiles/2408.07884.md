## Method Description

This paper proposes a step-by-step prompt design approach for survey generation: title → abstract → headings → content. The method decomposes survey generation into sequential steps where each step's output informs the next. First, a title is generated from the topic. Then, an abstract summarizing the scope. Then, a set of section headings organizing the topic structure. Finally, each section's content is generated based on the heading and the overall context.

The step-by-step decomposition reduces the complexity of generating a full survey in one pass, allowing the LLM to focus on one aspect at a time. Each step uses the outputs of previous steps as context, creating a coherent chain of generation decisions. The approach is prompt-based and does not require additional training or complex architectures.
