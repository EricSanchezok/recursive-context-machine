## Method Description

STORM (Synthesis of Topic Outlines through Retrieval and Question Asking) generates Wikipedia-style survey articles using a multi-perspective question-asking approach. The system: (1) decomposes the topic into multiple perspectives (e.g., historical, methodological, application-oriented), (2) generates specific questions from each perspective, (3) retrieves relevant information for each question, and (4) synthesizes a structured article from the collected information.

The multi-perspective approach ensures comprehensive coverage by forcing the system to consider the topic from different angles, preventing narrow treatment. The question-asking step generates targeted queries that retrieve specific information rather than generic topic descriptions. STORM produces well-structured, comprehensive articles organized by perspective.

## Category Rationale

STORM operates as a single pipeline with perspective decomposition, question generation, retrieval, and synthesis stages. The multi-perspective approach is handled by a single LLM.

## Limitations

- Designed for Wikipedia-style articles, not academic surveys
- No citation graph traversal — relies on web search
- Perspectives need to be predefined or LLM-generated with variable quality
- Question-driven retrieval may miss information that doesn't match any question
