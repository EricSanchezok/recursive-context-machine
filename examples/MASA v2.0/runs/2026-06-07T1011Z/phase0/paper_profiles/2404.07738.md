## Description

ResearchAgent is a multi-agent system for iterative research idea generation and literature review. The system uses an iterative generation-review cycle where an initial research idea or review draft is generated, reviewed by specialized reviewer agents, and refined based on feedback.

The architecture: (1) Generator Agent — produces initial research proposals or literature summaries; (2) Reviewer Agents — each critiques the generated output from a specific perspective (novelty, feasibility, coverage, citation accuracy); (3) Iterative Refinement — the Generator revises its output based on reviewer feedback, and the cycle repeats until quality criteria are met.

While focused on idea generation rather than survey writing, the iterative review-refine mechanism is directly applicable to survey generation workflows. The multi-perspective review ensures diverse feedback dimensions.
