## Method Description

SurGE (Survey Generation Evaluation) is a benchmark evaluating survey generation across four dimensions: coverage (whether the survey covers key papers and topics), referencing (accuracy and relevance of citations), structure (logical organization and clarity), and content quality (factual accuracy, readability, depth). The four dimensions provide a multi-faceted assessment that captures different aspects of survey quality.

The benchmark includes evaluation rubrics and example scoring for each dimension, enabling consistent and reproducible evaluation across different systems and research groups. The four dimensions provide a balanced assessment that goes beyond single-metric evaluations.
