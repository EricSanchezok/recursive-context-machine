## Method Description

This paper presents an interactive system for collaborative story generation where humans and AI work together through a plan-write-revise cycle. The system supports: (1) Planning — human and AI collaboratively outline the story structure, (2) Writing — the AI generates story text based on the plan, with human editing, and (3) Revising — the AI revises text based on human feedback, iterating until the human is satisfied.

While designed for story generation, the plan-write-revise cycle maps directly onto survey generation: humans could collaboratively plan the survey outline, have the AI generate sections, and iteratively revise based on human feedback on coverage, accuracy, and readability. The interactive cycle provides a model for human-AI collaboration in survey writing.
