## Method Description

This paper presents an interactive visualization system for supporting theme-based literature exploration. The system helps researchers develop effective reading strategies by organizing literature around themes rather than individual papers. Users can explore how themes evolve across papers, how different papers contribute to a theme, and how themes relate to each other.

The system provides interactive visualizations for thematic analysis, helping researchers identify key themes, track their development across papers, and understand the relationships between different research directions. It is designed as a reading aid rather than a survey generation system, supporting the human researcher in organizing their literature understanding thematically.
